# driving-and-camera

One line per gotcha.

- The scaffold car turns 1.6 rad/s at full lock, so 3 s of throttle plus steer passes heading PI; scenarios that check "x positive, z negative" after a turn use 1.5 s.
- Godot car port: a Node3D faces -Z at rotation 0, so `rotation.y = -heading` makes `-basis.z == forward(heading)`. The chase camera runs after the car with `process_physics_priority = 10`.
- Godot: the car steps on `SimClock.sim_tick(dt)`, not `_physics_process`, so time scale and the sim hold reach it. New stepping systems (physics, AI, missions) connect to `sim_tick` the same way.
- Sim clock is C1 (TECH.md "Main loop and time (C1)", built in P1-006, `game/core/sim_clock.gd`): one engine physics frame is one tick of 1/60 s, the time scale sets `Engine.time_scale` and `Engine.physics_ticks_per_second` together (`c1_rates`), and there is no accumulator any more. Godot 4.7.2 `PhysicsServer3D` has `set_active` but no manual space step, so game code cannot step physics by hand. Replays of one input script end at 0.0000 mm at scale 0.25, 1 and 4 and after holds (P0-017 bench and `game/tests/test_sim_clock.gd`); the old accumulator with free physics (C2) was off by up to 239 m. Unit tests drive a clock outside the tree with `run_frames(n)` (it never touches the engine there).
- Sim hold with physics (P0-017): call `PhysicsServer3D.set_active(false)` only at the start of an engine physics frame, right after the engine synced bodies to nodes (`SimClock.physics_frame()` is the only place that does it; `paused` is true at once, `server_stopped` from the next frame start). Stopped in the middle of a tick the tick's step is lost (bodies come back one step behind, 269 mm at speed); stopped between frames the body state sync stops too, so nodes show the step before until the release. Stage bodies under a hold: a body added while the clock runs falls a varying number of steps before the first tick, and replays then differ by one tick.
- GodotPhysics3D applies mass, inertia and centre of mass at the body's first step: an impulse in the tick a body is made acts on 1 kg (the V2 car left at 185 m/s). Jolt applies them at once. On both engines `get_inverse_inertia_tensor()` reads identity until the first state sync. Start vehicle forces one tick after creation.
- VehicleBody3D (4.7.2, probed): drives along local +Z and a positive `steering` turns left, so a car that faces -Z needs the body turned half a turn about Y (a rotation, never a mirror). Its wheel ray starts one radius above the connection point and the hit distance is scaled by raylen / (raylen + radius): with the KEEP tune it rides 45 mm lower than the numbers say (0.327 m against 0.372 m). `brake` is an impulse cap per wheel and step, `engine_force` is newtons per traction wheel; each wheel has its own `engine_force`, `brake` and `steering`. Input shows in the motion after 2 ticks (our V2: 1).
- Step-up for a CharacterBody3D capsule (P0-017 `walker.gd`): try the move one step higher with `test_move` (up, forward, down). The round foot lands on the step edge, whose normal is too steep to count as floor: judge the landing with a ray just ahead of the foot, and allow a step within 3 ticks of leaving the floor. Without the second rule sprinting up stairs lost 1 to 2 ticks per step (0.2 s windows at 71 percent of the speed).
- Jolt builds a mesh collider when its body enters the space, on the main thread: 0.6 to 1.3 microseconds per triangle (315,000 triangles 179 ms, 20,800 17 ms, 1,300 1.7 ms). Shape and `PhysicsServer3D` calls from a WorkerThreadPool thread are safe (240 loads, no error) but only queued (0.01 ms on the thread). With `physics/3d/run_on_separate_thread` the attach call takes 0.04 to 0.2 ms and the next frame waits for the build instead (17 ms of build shows as a 9.5 ms frame, 179 ms as 177 ms), and `direct_space_state` only answers inside physics frames. So cut tile colliders into chunks of about 1,300 triangles and attach one per frame.
- Measuring physics (P0-017): the server step is the gap between the last physics callback of one step and the first of the next inside the same rendered frame, so it needs 2 or more steps per frame (time scale 4). Jolt reports 0 for the `Performance` monitors `PHYSICS_3D_ACTIVE_OBJECTS`, `COLLISION_PAIRS` and `ISLAND_COUNT`, and its memory is not in `OS.get_static_memory_usage()` (read the process size with ps in a fresh process). `PhysicalBone3D` has no `sleeping` property (read `BODY_STATE_SLEEPING` from the server). A script loop measured alone is cold: V2 `sim_step` reads 74 microseconds for one car and 21 per car among 30.
- Physics numbers on the M2 Pro (P0-017, `harness/reports/arch/physics.json` `sections`, Jolt unless said): step p95 0.74 ms for 200 static and 30 dynamic bodies (GodotPhysics3D 0.56), 1000 rays 2.0 ms (GodotPhysics3D 3.0); KEEP tune on V1 and V2 alike: 0 to 100 km/h in 6.1 s, top 106 km/h, 100 to 0 in 28.2 m, full lock radius 6.7 to 7.0 m at 38 km/h and 21 m at 77 km/h; a physics car costs 0.035 ms per tick (V2) or 0.030 (V1); a character 0.032 ms; a falling ragdoll 0.06 ms; 200 pedestrians plus 30 traffic agents in GDScript 0.9 ms all at once, 0.7 ms in turns; nothing changes 7000 m from the origin.
- Only the clock and `*camera.gd` files may have a `_physics_process` outside `game/bench/` (test in `game/tests/test_sim_clock.gd`). A node that only needs to mark the physics frame start connects to `get_tree().physics_frame` instead (it fires before every `_physics_process`; the debug frame probe does this).
- V2 staging (P1-020): freeze a vehicle with FREEZE_MODE_STATIC, never KINEMATIC, and set the transform on the server too (`PhysicsServer3D.body_set_state`). Jolt moves a kinematic body to a new transform only in the next step and the node reads the old one back meanwhile: a teleported car looked back to where it came from for a frame, the streaming focus followed it and the teleport answered on the old ring.
- Rays that look for ground (world_stream `ground_at`, stream probes, the vehicle's wheels and settle, the chase camera) use collision mask 1: the V2 car body is layer 2 and a default mask (all layers) hits its roof.
- Godot's `Performance.TIME_PHYSICS_PROCESS` is the max over the last second, updated once a second: never average it per tick. Time a physics iteration as the gap between two `physics_frame` signals inside one drawn frame (windowed, time scale 4), as `vehicle('cost')` does.
- V2 car feel (P1-020): with `linear_damp` 0.05 the drag is about 24 N per km/h (1.4 m/s2 of slowdown at 100 km/h) and it, not the engine, set the old 106 km/h top; 0.02 gives about 9.6 N per km/h. Shift up at or before the gear's fade starts (`shift_at` <= 1 - `fade_share`), or the fade eats the force the shift needs and the car stalls in a gear. A short fade (`fade_share` 0.06) on the last gear pins the top speed within a km/h whatever the drag.
- The bench course's ramp top (a sharp 6 percent crest) throws the car for about 18 ticks at 100 km/h: the flyover probe counts its joint window from 10 m before the first joint, the crest ticks apart (`lossTicksBefore`).
- A P-only speed hold (throttle = error x 0.25) holds 2 to 3 km/h under its target in a full lock turn: use gain 1 or more in probes, or the lock radius is measured at the wrong speed.
- Chase camera: sweep a sphere (cast_motion, 0.2 m) from the pivot to the camera, not a ray: a lerping camera grazing a wall top passes a ray in its own frame and is behind the wall in the next one.
