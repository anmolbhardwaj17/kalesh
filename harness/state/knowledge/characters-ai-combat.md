# characters-ai-combat

Characters, AI and combat: one line per gotcha, added by builders as they hit them (area in docs/READ_MAP.md "Characters, AI and combat").
- Step-up (P1-022 `on_foot.gd`): call `test_move` with the body's own `safe_margin` (0.01), not the default 0.001. On a riser edge `move_and_slide` moves along the tilted floor normal of the edge underfoot and meets the next edge, while a flat 0.001 test just misses it: the step never fires and the body stops dead on the stairs. Test 2 cm past the move too.
- Jolt `test_move` from a body resting on an edge lists that edge first with an up normal: ask for several collisions (`max_collisions` 4) and treat the move as a step when any contact is steep or more than 3 cm above the feet.
- A walk move (2.7 cm a tick) that first touches a kerb edge lands back on its own tread after the up-forward-down test (under 2 mm higher), so the body stops at the kerb for good; try longer forward moves (0.06, then 0.1 m) only when the short one fails, or every gait gains 5 percent speed on stairs.
- Jump arc in ticks: move by the mean of the vertical speeds at the start and end of the tick (`foot_motor.gd` `fall`), and the apex is exactly 0.45 m after 18 ticks; plain `v -= g dt` then `y += v dt` peaks at 0.425 m.
- Crouch a stand-in capsule by changing the `CapsuleMesh.height`, not the node's Y scale: a scaled capsule has oval caps (reads as a squashed shape in a shot).
- The on-foot camera sweeps with mask 3 (world and vehicles): with mask 1 only it can sit inside a parked car's roof right after getting out. Fading needs a mesh per wall: the camera fades the MeshInstance3D children of a hit collider (or its `fade_meshes` metadata), so walls merged into one course mesh cannot fade.
- AnimationNodeTransition: a `transition_request` set before the AnimationTree has processed once is dropped, and the Transition then plays no input: the body stands in the rest pose (a T). Advance the tree once (`advance(0)`) before the first request (`humanoid_anim.gd` `_build_tree`).
- The UAL gait clips do not fit any ground speed (P2-041): the walk is a 0.86 m/s stroll; the jog and sprint bound (hips travel 26 and 15 cm, a foot is down 17 to 23 percent of the cycle and crosses the ground at 4.9 and 8.2 m/s there). The old stride figure (the lower ball's mean speed over the whole cycle, flight included) hid this: it read 3 percent slide for the jog. The game plays `rig/loco_clips.res` instead (built by `tools/characters/loco_build.mjs`, rate 1 at each gait's speed for a body scale of 0.94, 0.966 to 1.035 for the three leads); foot contact is judged by height (`foot_contact.gd`). Change a gait speed in `foot_motor.gd` and the clips must be rebuilt (`GAITS` in `characters/tools/build_locomotion.gd`).
- Godot's import strips a `_Loop` / `-loop` name suffix and sets the loop mode: the UAL clip `Walk_Loop` is `Walk` in the game. Track paths after the humanoid retarget are `%GeneralSkeleton:<ProfileBone>`; the AnimationTree's root_node must be the node that owns that unique-named skeleton.
- Clothes on the stand-in mannequin are painted in `rig/lead_look.gdshader` from the rest pose (CUSTOM0), never separate meshes: nothing can poke through the body. Hem and belt tests must cover the leg region too (the hips' sides follow the thigh bones), or the shirt reads as briefs.
- Unzipping a name with [ or ] (the UAL zips): unzip reads them as a wildcard set; escape them (`\[`).
- The on-foot camera never turns on its own: a scripted walk must press `look_x` toward the walk (`lib/sim.js walk` does, turnCamera) or the player runs across the frame and the shot shows a side view.
- Out of a seat (P1-024): Jolt moves a kinematic body to a new transform only during the next step, so right after `place()` the character's body is still inside the vehicle; with collisions back on, the recovery pulled it into the car (x 1.45 to 0.51 in one tick, then thrown 20 to 100 m on the next F). `seat(false)` now collides with the world only (layer 0, mask 1) for UNSEAT_TICKS (3), then the full layer and mask.
- Never hold the brake pedal on a vehicle at a standstill to park it: below 1 km/h the brake is reverse (drive_input.gd). PlayerMode's exit reversed the car at 2.95 km/h, just under EXIT_KMH; `player_mode.gd _stop()` now brakes only while rolling forward and lets the parking hold keep it still.
- The library's clips stand 8 cm over the skeleton's origin (the crouch set 4.3 cm): `humanoid_anim.gd` lowers the model by `loco_clips.res` meta `ground`, the build lifts the crouch clips onto the same plane. A new library clip that stands at another height sinks or floats: lift it in `build_moves.gd` (`_reground`).
- AnimationNodeTransition fades between two inputs only: a request made while a cross fade runs drops the fading input at once (the hips jumped 19 cm in a tick on move, air, land, move at a kerb). `humanoid_anim.gd _set_input` refuses a request during a fade unless it cannot wait (vault, seat); callers ask again next tick.
- Baked clips: 60 keys a second is too few for a sprinting leg (it turns 8 degrees between keys, and the straight line between keys strays 7 mm, which reads as 10 percent foot slide when sampled between keys). The built clips have 120 keys a second; a bone that never moves gets one key.
- Measure a planted foot at the game's tick, not finer: sampled at 800 Hz the same clip reads 10 percent slide from the wobble between keys, at 60 Hz 0.3 percent.
- Foot placement (`body_feel.gd`): ease heights in world space, not relative to the capsule (it hops 15 to 18 cm in a tick at a kerb or riser, and rides about one riser higher than the tread under its middle on stairs). The hips go by the lowest planted foot's ground, never over the capsule, at most 4 cm a tick down plus the capsule's own eased fall. A planted foot can still drift a few centimetres over an edge (the capsule does not move exactly as the animation strides; a step-up carries it up to 10 cm in a tick): a world hold of the planted ankle was tried and left out (it made more feet miss); it needs its own task.
- A fall shorter than 14 ticks (a kerb, a stair at a run) must keep the gait animation: the jump loop for a 15 cm drop was the largest pop on foot (`on_foot.gd FALL_ANIM_TICKS`).
- Leaning the whole body costs the back leg its reach: the sprint's extra lean bends the spine only; the acceleration lean turns the hips about the ground point under them and the feet are solved back.
- `foot('cost')` under a loaded host (load average over 10, a bake running) reads about 25 percent high (0.050 to 0.055 ms per character against 0.034 ms in a quiet run): rerun it alone on a quiet machine before treating it as a regression.
