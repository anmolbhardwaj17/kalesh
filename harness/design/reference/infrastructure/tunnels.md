# Tunnels and vehicle underpass boxes

Tags: [S] source, [P] read from a photo in img/, [E] estimate from standard practice (see README.md). Written 2026-11-13.

Delhi has one true road tunnel (the Pragati Maidan main tunnel, opened 2022) plus a family of box underpasses built with it, older flyover-era underpasses (see `underpasses_subways.md`) and small building passages in the old city. Metro tunnels are in `metro.md`, rail cuttings in `rail.md`.

## TUN-T1 Pragati Maidan main tunnel (2022, the newest and brightest)

- **Where.** Pilot: Mathura Road and Purana Qila Road under the Pragati Maidan exhibition grounds, from the Ring Road and ITO side (near Bhairon Marg) to the India Gate end. In the bake it is the OSM way set "Pragati Maidan Tunnel" (secondary, 10 ways, about 2.5 km of tunnel pieces including links). See variant_map.md. [S] route Ring Road to India Gate via Purana Qila Road.
- **Type.** Cut and cover rectangular box, six lane divided, with two cross tunnels under the main road that let traffic reach the basement parking from either side [S: ThePrint 2022]. Length quoted as 1.3 to 1.6 km depending on whether the underpasses are counted [S]. A contractor page gives width 28.2 to 42 m and calls it India's widest urban road tunnel [S, weak]; Swarajya says the main tunnel is 27 m wide [S, snippet].
- **Cross section for the kit.** Two carriageways of 3 lanes at 3.5 m each (10.5 m) with a 0.5 m edge strip and a 0.75 to 1 m raised side walkway on the outer wall; central dividing wall about 0.5 m thick with openings at cross points. Clear height 5.5 m minimum (the IRC road clearance rule) [E]. In the photo the walls rise to a flat soffit about 6 to 7 m over the road [P].
- **Walls.** Painted concrete, not tile. Lower 1.2 m is a black dado. Above it a 3.5 to 4 m tall mural band runs the full length: coral red, navy and white swirling pattern, with a different panel (teal and green knot pattern on pink) at the cross openings and the portals. The contractor says about 70,000 m2 of art showing Indian festivals, rituals and seasons [S weak]. Mural zone ends at a plain grey strip, about 1.5 m, where the cable trays run [P: tunnel_pragati_maidan_main.jpg].
- **Ceiling.** Bare grey concrete soffit with two long cable trays and a red-pink conduit pipe along the right wall top. No false ceiling, no jet fans visible in the photo (ventilation is "modern" per ThePrint [S]).
- **Lighting.** White LED downlights in pairs, one fixture every 6 to 8 m on both wall tops and extra pairs mid-ceiling, cool white 5000 to 6000 K, very bright, no sodium [P].
- **Road surface and markings.** Asphalt, dark grey, white solid edge lines and white dashed lane lines, no cat's eyes seen. Kerb along the wall is a painted black and white stripe (every 0.5 m) [P].
- **Signage.** Yellow square chevron signs on the wall at the curves, a hanging variable message sign box (black, amber text, "N.H.A.I." plate) every 80 to 100 m, small illuminated lane signs, emergency telephones and fire hose points on the right wall [P+E].
- **Safety systems.** Smart fire management, ventilation, automated drainage, digitally controlled CCTV, a public announcement system [S: ThePrint]. For the kit: visible CCTV domes every 50 m, fire hydrant cabinets (red) at 60 m, exit doors at the cross tunnels.
- **Portals.** Open U ramps with RCC retaining walls and painted bands in the same mural colours, edge railings in the Delhi orange or grey, grill barrier at the top [E].
- **Wear.** New (2022): clean walls, light tyre dust at the kerb, the mural base slightly scuffed. Wear 0.1 to 0.2.
- **Owner photos (2026-11-14, current, press photos kept outside git in `harness/design/reference/owner/pragati_maidan_tunnel/`).** The main stretches look different from the 2025 photo above: inside, a green base, yellow walls and a wave band of yellow, ochre and green with birds and leaves; a cream soffit with two continuous white LED strip lines near the wall tops, orange marigold strings across, cable trays, jet fans, red fire point boxes, black and white kerbs; the wide section has a central line of pillars painted in tall saffron, purple, teal and yellow panels with mandala patterns on yellow and white striped plinths with uplights; the approach ramp walls are painted end to end (cream with flower discs, a blue river band, lotus, swans, trees, a saffron band on top, green at the bottom). The kit (P2-032) follows these.
- **Game kit.** A 6 to 7 m high box, 27 to 30 m wide, mural texture tiles every 8 m, light fixture row, cable tray strip, VMS box, chevron signs. Mood: bright, safe, almost like a showroom. Use it as the contrast to old flyover underpasses.

## TUN-T2 Pragati Maidan box underpasses (2022 family)

- **Where.** Five or six underpasses built with the corridor: four on Mathura Road, one on Bhairon Marg, one at the Ring Road and Bhairon Marg junction near ITO [S: ThePrint]. In the bake the corridor appears as "Pragati Maidan Tunnel" primary (4 ways, 398 m); the two OSM "Underpass" link ways (973071350 primary_link 568 m at about (28.6129, 77.2512), 1322467992 secondary_link 452 m at about (28.6035, 77.2402)) lie just outside the baked outline [S: OSM, variant_map.md].
- **Type.** RCC box underpasses, ramp down and up; some were built by pushing precast boxes under the railway line (Underpass 5 on Bhairon Marg) [S: ThePrint]. After a precast box sank in a flood, the redesign narrows the one side to 6.25 m wide and 3.9 m high, light motor vehicles only (was 11.5 m wide, 5.5 m high) [S: Daily Pioneer, snippet].
- **Finish.** Painted concrete with the same dado and mural scheme as T1 on the longer ones; short boxes have plain light grey paint with a black dado and sodium or white LED strip lights [E]. Height signs ("3.9 m" limit bars) hang at each entrance for the LMV only box [S/E].
- **Game kit.** Same kit as T1 with two profiles: standard 2 lane (9 m wide, 5.5 m high) and LMV only (6.25 m wide, 3.9 m high). Height limiter bar (yellow and black) at the entrance. Drain gratings across the road at both ramp bottoms and a pump house kerbside.

## TUN-T3 Basement and service tunnels (parking links)

- **Where.** OSM "service tunnel" ways under Pragati Maidan (about 1.85 km of tunnel pieces, layer -1) and the Palika Bazaar parking ("Palika Parking", underground car park) [S: OSM]. These are the Pragati Maidan basement connections and the CP Palika underground parking.
- **Type.** Low underground roads and ramps with painted concrete, 3.0 to 3.5 m clearance, one lane or two narrow lanes, ramps at 1:10 [E].
- **Finish.** Painted grey concrete, yellow and black bands at columns, painted bay numbers, fluorescent strip lights every 4 m, a few exposed pipes, concrete floor with oil stains. Entrance: yellow height bar, boom barrier, a small booth [E].
- **Game kit.** Do not use the T1 look. This is the cheap, practical basement: low ceiling, strip lights, column bands.

## TUN-T4 Building passages in the old city

- **Where.** OSM `tunnel=building_passage` ways: Chandni Chowk and Fatehpuri side, Paharganj and some Lutyens residential blocks (about 15 short pieces in the pilot, 15 to 80 m each); Lothian Road and Gokhale Road in Civil Lines are named examples [S: OSM].
- **Type.** A road or lane that passes under a building: 3 to 5 m wide, 2.5 to 3.8 m high for bikes and handcarts, sometimes a taller arch gate for trucks [E].
- **Finish.** Plastered brick or exposed brick with a pointed or flat arch, painted in worn yellow, cream or pale green; overhead cables, bulbs or bare tube lights, a shrine niche, shopfront shutters opening into the passage, bicycles and scooters parked along the wall [E].
- **Game kit.** One arch and one flat lintel profile, brick tiles with plaster loss, shop shutter strips on one side, a bare bulb every 6 m. Each passage gets its own paint colour.

## Not built here (listed so nobody invents them)

- A news headline names a "Bhagwan Das-Purana Qila tunnel project"; it reads as the same Pragati Maidan corridor, and the OSM data shows no second road tunnel in the pilot [E, headline only: https://indianinfrastructure.com/?p=88785].
- Delhi has no mountain tunnels. Do not add tunnel portals with rock faces.
