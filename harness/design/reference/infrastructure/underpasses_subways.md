# Underpasses and pedestrian subways

Tags: [S] source, [P] read from a photo in img/, [E] estimate (see README.md). Written 2026-11-13.

Two families. Road underpasses (UND-U*) carry cars under a junction or a rail line. Pedestrian subways (SUB-S*) are the walk-under crossings and underground bazaars, the most Delhi thing in this folder: Connaught Place has a whole network.

## UND-U1 Flyover-era road underpass (painted concrete, drips and sodium light)

- **Where.** Pilot: the Ring Road underpass at Yamuna Bazar by the Hanuman Mandir flyover complex (OSM tertiary tunnel, layer -1, ways 175800331 and 292171525, about 254 m) [S: OSM]. Outside the pilot but the same family: Ring Road at Dhaula Kuan (RTR), Benito Juarez Marg, Moolchand, AIIMS side [S: Wikipedia Ring Road list].
- **Type.** RCC box with sloped ramps in open cut and RCC retaining walls, 2 lanes each way split by a central wall or a pier line, hump cover at the ramp top to stop rain flowing in [S: HT snippet on the Benito Juarez Marg underpass, a Y shaped one way underpass of about 1.2 km that opened in July 2022].
- **Cross section for the kit.** Carriageway 7.5 m per direction, 0.5 m edge strips, 5.5 m clear height [E]. Ramp length about 120 to 150 m each side at 1 in 25 to 1 in 20 [E]. Walls vertical RCC 6 to 6.5 m high at the middle, ramp retaining walls step down to 0.
- **Walls and ceiling.** Painted cement concrete (cream or light grey), lower 1 m black dado, panel joints every 5 m, faint white ceiling slab with exposed cable tray. Hindustan Times found one such underpass (RTR, Dhaula Kuan) damp, dark, with leaking walls and a damaged road within three years of opening [S: HT snippet]. Kit wear 0.5 to 0.7 for the old ones, 0.2 for 2022 ones.
- **Lighting.** Older: sodium lamps (amber, 3 to 4 m apart) in wall tops, many dead. Newer: LED strips. Both get a brighter pool at the ramp entry [E].
- **Dressing.** Yellow and black painted kerb, white edge line, a "Height 5.5 m" sign, drain gratings at the bottom, a pump house, poster and stain streaks on the retaining walls, painted warning chevrons at the nose of the central wall [E].

## UND-U2 Road under a masonry rail arch: Minto Bridge

- **Where.** Minto Bridge (officially Shivaji Bridge), Vivekananda Road (once Minto Road), between Connaught Place and Ajmeri Gate, under the Northern Railway line into New Delhi station. In the bake the rail pieces are the layer 1 bridge ways of the Mumbai and Howrah lines near (28.633, 77.2267), with the road under them [S: Wikipedia Minto Bridge; bake].
- **Type.** A railway underbridge built 1926 to 1933 to carry the new line into New Delhi; sources call it a large segmental arch of brick masonry with lime mortar and cement [S: Wikipedia, Down To Earth snippet]. A heritage structure. It is known for waterlogging since independence; the PWD built a new drainage pipeline in 2022 [S].
- **Kit.** Arched brick soffit about 6 m high at the crown over a 2 lane road and two footpaths, red brick and sandstone coping, a black rail bridge deck above with ballast showing at the parapet, a yellow height gauge, a low point with drains. Weather mark: dark water line on the walls. The road dips into a shallow dish in the middle [E].
- **Wear.** 0.7: blackened brick, plaster loss, painted stripes at the edge, hanging cables.

## SUB-S1 Connaught Place outer circle subways

- **Where.** Around the CP outer circle, linking the inner and outer rings across the traffic: openings at Janpath market, KG Marg, M and N Block, Palika Bazaar and Barakhamba / Panchkuian Road sides; OSM shows a dense network of layer -1, -2 and -4 ways around (28.629 to 28.635, 77.216 to 77.224) mixing subway and metro concourse links [S: Deccan Herald subways piece, NDMC records, OSM].
- **History and condition.** Old subways of the CP circle; NDMC proposals (2007 to 2013) planned eight new ones and escalators, and traffic police allowed only four (two at Panchkuian Road and two at Barakhamba Road) [S: NDMC papers and CWG blog, snippets]. NDMC approved Rs 6.08 crore to replace seven non-working escalators [S: ThePrint, snippet, date not shown]. The redevelopment also specified "mandana" stone and granite flooring and better subway lighting [S: NDMC council papers, snippet].
- **Cross section.** About 4 to 6 m wide, 2.6 to 3 m high corridors, with 3 m wide stair flights, a central or side steel pipe railing, an escalator and a stair side by side in the busiest ones [E].
- **Finish.** Wall tiles or painted cement plaster in cream and ochre, matching the colonnade, ceiling with recessed fluorescent tubes, floor of grey granite or mandana stone slabs with a darker border, shop kiosks (small closed shutters) in the corridor, public toilet and police booth near the main subways [E]. Signage in Hindi and English with arrows to blocks.
- **Wear.** 0.5: dull lamps, stained plaster, taped holes in tiles, some escalators dead.
- **Kit notes.** Stair flight prefab with steel rails; straight corridor tile; T junction tile; entrance kiosk above ground with a low canopy and railing, the way Delhi subways show a stair mouth in a pavement island with a pipe railing.

## SUB-S2 Palika Bazaar (underground market)

- **Where.** Under Central Park / inner circle of Connaught Place; OSM way 1393736849 (landuse retail, rooms at layer -1 and -2) and the Palika Parking [S: OSM, harness/data/underground.json].
- **Facts.** Set up in 1979 [S: Wikipedia]. 380 numbered shops, about 15,000 people at any time, electronics and clothes [S]. Seven gates: 1 main staircase facing Central Park; 2 and 7 handicap ramps; 4 and 5 spiral staircase entries; 6 a staircase; 3 closed [S: Wikipedia list]. The NDMC has a regeneration plan because the market is run down [S].
- **Interior look.** Round columns clad in green glazed tile (about 0.9 m across), polished grey stone floor, a ring-shaped raised ceiling cove with fluorescent tubes and a white suspended ceiling, wide aisles, shop fronts in glass and steel with roll shutters, hanging signboards in blue, red and white; busy crowd [P: subway_palika_bazaar.jpg]. Ceiling about 3.2 m [E].
- **Entrances.** Surface kiosks with a spiral staircase (a drum with a rail) and one ramp, in the CP lawns; cream plaster walls, rusted railings [E].
- **Kit.** One interior room kit (column ring, cove ceiling tile, shopfront strip) and three entrance types. Wear 0.7. Respect rule: the market is a real place of livelihood; no jokes about counterfeits in the dressing.

## SUB-S3 Standard civic pedestrian subway (white tile and mural panel)

- **Where.** Ring Road and Mathura Road crossings (the photographed example is Pragati Maidan at Bhairon Marg, 2014), Tis Hazari (Rajiv Agrawal Paidal Par Path, OSM way 1498275699) and similar PWD subways [S: OSM; P].
- **Look.** White ceramic wall tiles from the dado up to the ceiling, with a bold mural panel (a colourful relief or mosaic of local motifs, about 3 m wide), a black and grey chequered tile floor, flat white ceiling with a few fluorescent strips, steel white painted gates at the head of stairs, a wide ramp beside the stairs. Floor stained, unlit parts dark [P: subway_pragati_maidan_bhairon_marg.jpg].
- **Size.** In the photo the subway is wide (about 12 m) with a flat ceiling about 3 m; ordinary ones are 4 to 6 m wide [P+E].
- **Wear.** 0.5 to 0.8: cracked tiles, dark patches, paan stains at corners, the odd closed gate. A police booth or lift at the larger ones.

## SUB-S4 Kartavya Path pedestrian underpasses (2022)

- **Where.** Four new underpasses built along Kartavya Path (formerly Rajpath), at the busy junctions including where Janpath crosses and the India Gate end [S: Deccan Chronicle 2022-09-08]. In OSM: "Janpath South underpass" (way 1548439816, 52 m) and "Janpath North underpass" (way 1548439829, 52 m), layer -1, plus unnamed crossings around (28.611 to 28.614, 77.226 to 77.235) [S: OSM].
- **Material.** Not described in the sources. The avenue's red granite walkways cover over 15.5 km [S: Quint, snippet], so the kit uses red granite floor and a sandstone-toned wall, a white ceiling with linear LED, glass or stainless handrails, lifts at the ends [E, to verify with a photo].
- **Cross section.** 6 to 8 m wide, 3 to 3.5 m high, step free with ramps [E].
- **Wear.** 0.05 to 0.1, new, clean, bright. This is the polished end of the range.

## SUB-S5 Station links (New Delhi station, metro concourse corridors)

- **Where.** OSM layer -1 ways at New Delhi railway station and the Airport Express terminal, about (28.644, 77.222 to 77.224), 687 m in total; and Rajiv Chowk and Central Secretariat concourse corridors [S: OSM].
- **Look.** Metro corridors: white and light grey metal panel ceilings, vitrified tile floor with yellow tactile strips, stainless steel handrails, wide glass kiosk fronts, blue and white direction signs. Railway subway links: painted brick and cement, steel railings, rolling shutter shops [P: metro_underground_station.jpg for the metro type; E for the railway type].
