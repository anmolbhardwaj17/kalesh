# Foot over bridges and skywalks

Tags: [S] source, [P] read from a photo in img/, [E] estimate (see README.md). Written 2026-11-13.

The pilot has 24 foot bridge features in the bake plus the long chained New Delhi station network [S: bake, health.json graph counts: 24 footBridges]. They are all the same job (get people over traffic or tracks) in four looks.

## FOB-B1 New Delhi station foot over bridges (arched steel, the showpiece)

- **Where.** New Delhi railway station: the FOB over the station forecourt and Paharganj approach and the chain of platform bridges (OSM way 34404746 is 50 foot way pieces, 1.8 km in total; way 80414535 is the main 576 m span; "Foot Over Bridge 1" way 80414542, 264 m, "Foot Over Bridge 3" way 403831592, 117 m) at (28.642 to 28.644, 77.2195 to 77.2215) [S: OSM, bake]. The 242 m skywalk joins the FOB to the metro station [S: Wikipedia New Delhi station].
- **Look.** A long closed bridge with a tubular steel frame of repeated oval and arch ribs, silver-grey, with perforated or glass infill in the arches and a closed roof; a slim circular or Y shaped steel column at the middle, escalators and stairs at each end; mixed with older red-brown brick and plaster buildings nearby [P: fob_new_delhi_station_arches.jpg].
- **Dimensions.** Deck about 4.5 to 6 m wide in the photo, 3 m internal clear height, arch ribs 6 to 8 m apart, deck 6 to 7 m above the forecourt [E].
- **Wear.** 0.3: dusty arch faces, pigeons.

## FOB-B2 Railway platform foot over bridge (steel frame under a sheet roof)

- **Where.** Delhi Junction (Old Delhi) and Sadar Bazar (OSM ways 1287299124, 1287299130 (153 and 161 m), 464312306 (164 m)), and the platform bridges of New Delhi [S: OSM].
- **Look.** A steel truss walkway on steel columns that rise from the platforms, with stairs at the platform heads (concrete treads, steel pipe rails, mesh cladding), a blue-sheet or grey roof joining the platform roofs; hanging signboards, information displays, pigeon nets [P: rail_new_delhi_platform_roofs.jpg shows the blue roofs and the steel frame; E for details].
- **Dimensions.** Clear width 3 to 4 m, headroom 2.4 m, deck 6.5 to 7 m above rail level, stairs 1.8 to 2.5 m wide; some old stairs lose a tread [E].
- **Wear.** 0.7: painted steel rusting at bolts, spit marks, a few missing lamps.

## FOB-B3 ITO Skywalk and modern PWD skywalks

- **Where.** ITO junction ("W Point" crossing): OSM way 854903774 (547 m) is the skywalk way; way 1448561131 (167 m) is a branch; way 814940336 (51 m) and way 998790348 (93 m) are links near Hans Bhawan [S: OSM, bake].
- **Facts.** Main skywalk 400 m long and 5 m wide, a loop and ramp section of 130 m at 3 m wide, seven glass lifts for 20 people each, steel structure with tensile fabric roofing; the Hans Bhawan foot over bridge is 54 m long and 5 m wide; designed by Design Forum International for the Delhi PWD and opened in October 2018 [S: Archello, Daily Pioneer, MGS Architecture, search snippets; the official opening is dated 15 October 2018 in the Ministry release photos on Commons].
- **Look.** A white and grey steel deck on slim tree-like or circular columns, white membrane (tent) roof cut into curves, glass and steel lift towers at the junctions, LED lighting along the handrail, a red granite or tile floor, wide ramps [E, from the facts above; no photo saved].
- **Wear.** 0.15: clean steel, some stains on the membrane.

## FOB-B4 PWD steel road FOB (stairs and lift)

- **Where.** The common type across the pilot: Ring Road at Red Fort and Loha Pul (OSM ways 80429832 and 80429897, 66 and 104 m), Shivaji Stadium and Minto Road (370765173, 95 m), Mandi House and ITO side (370765177, 76 m), plus small links (35 to 65 m): ways 1248031156, 1248079213, 1294941406, 1429504126, 200251460, 435426345 [S: OSM, bake].
- **Structure.** Steel members of structural plates and rolled sections, IS 2062 grade Fe 410 WA; stair and lift towers with electrical installation; stainless steel grade 304 railings; typical tender estimate Rs 3 crore, six month build [S: Delhi PWD tenders for Majnu ka Tilla, Katwaria Sarai, Okhla Mandi, search snippets 2026-11-13]. New ones are made disabled friendly with lifts as well as stairs [S: HT 2019 snippet].
- **Look.** A rectangular box or open truss deck over the road on one or two tubular steel columns, painted grey, blue or yellow, mesh or perforated panel sides, a fabric or sheet roof, concrete stairs in a U at each end with steel railings, a glass lift in some, hoarding or advertising panels on the side; lamps along the underside [E].
- **Dimensions.** Span 25 to 40 m over 6 to 8 lanes, clear walkway 2.5 to 3.5 m, headroom 2.4 m, soffit 5.5 m above the carriageway, stair rise 150 mm, 35 to 40 steps up [E].
- **Wear.** 0.5 for older ones: faded paint, broken glass in lifts (lifts often shut), peeling posters. The Delhi High Court has criticised the poor condition of existing FOBs; PWD plans 21 new ones [S: Construction World, The Week snippets].
- **Kit.** A deck module of 4 m long with side panels, truss support, two stair types (straight and U), lift tower, roof variant (open, sheet, membrane), a hoarding variant.

## FOB-B5 Older RCC or steel stair FOB (the plain old one)

- **Where.** Ring Road and arterial crossings throughout the pilot's older streets (Chandni Chowk side, Paharganj); short pieces (8 to 20 m) in OSM ways 264770688, 311524765, 77614294 [S: OSM].
- **Look.** A narrow steel walkway of 2 m with a pipe railing, a concrete stair with painted tread edges (yellow), hoardings, no roof, often with hawkers on the landing [E].
