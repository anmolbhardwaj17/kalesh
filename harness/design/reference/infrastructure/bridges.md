# Bridges: Yamuna crossings, rail bridges, road over rail

Tags: [S] source, [P] read from a photo in img/, [E] estimate (see README.md). Written 2026-11-13.

Flyovers are in `flyovers.md`; the metro viaduct and its river bridge are in `metro.md`. This file covers river bridges, bridges that carry railways, roads over railways, and barrages.

## RBR-Y1 Loha Pul (Old Yamuna Bridge, "Iron Bridge", bridge number 249)

- **Where.** Between Old Delhi and Shahdara, at about (28.664, 77.2495). Baked as OSM way 77603110 "Old Iron Bridge (Loha Pul)" (rail, layer 2) and way 77603072, only the west end 37 m lies inside the baked outline; the full bridge is about 800 m. The new rail bridge (OSM "New Yamuna Rail Bridge (u/c)", way 80429909, 1.27 km) is being built beside it [S: OSM].
- **Facts.** Built 1863 to 1866 by East Indian Railway for 1,616,335 pounds, opened 1867; double deck steel truss; total length 804.67 m, 12 spans of 61.72 m (202.5 ft) [S: Wikipedia]. Upper deck carries the railway (Old Delhi to Shahdara), lower deck carries road traffic [S: Wikipedia]. The railway was single track, doubled in 1913 or 1934 (sources disagree) [S]. A new 992.25 m bridge is being built next to it [S].
- **Look.** Riveted steel truss painted dark and heavily rusted, rust brown to orange (#7a4a2e to #9c5a32). Polygonal top chord that rises over each pier with vertical posts and X bracing; deck girders below. Small steel refuge platforms with a railing cantilever from the truss at every pier, where pedestrians can step aside. Overhead line masts stand on the top deck, with insulator arms; trees grow around the abutments [P: bridge_loha_pul_01.jpg, bridge_loha_pul_03.jpg].
- **Piers.** Brick and stone masonry piers, rectangular with pointed cutwaters, red-brown brick and grey stone, vegetation in the joints; on the east bank the abutment is brick with stone coping [P].
- **Dimensions for the kit.** Span 61.7 m, truss depth about 8 to 9 m, total height deck to water about 12 to 14 m, rail deck width 5.5 to 6.5 m for two tracks, road deck 6 m [E].
- **Wear.** 0.9: rust streaks, peeling paint, bird droppings, graffiti on the abutments, bulbs on the road deck.
- **Hero landmark.** Treat as a signature piece of the Old Delhi riverbank. Keep silhouette accurate.

## BRI-Y1 Modern Yamuna road bridge (ITO and Vikas Marg crossing)

- **Where.** The pilot's Vikas Marg crossing of the Yamuna (OSM "Vikas Marg" bridge ways 70255387 and others, about 770 m of primary road bridge and 386 m of secondary) at the ITO barrage, near (28.628, 77.252) [S: OSM, bake]. Same family: Nizamuddin Bridge and the Geeta Colony bridges on the Ring Road (outside the pilot) [E].
- **Structure.** Multi-span precast prestressed concrete girder bridge, spans 30 to 40 m, 3 or 4 lanes per direction plus footways; wall-type or twin-column piers in the river, with rounded cutwaters (about 2 m thick, 8 m wide) on a pile cap in the water [E].
- **Edge.** Concrete parapet 1.1 m high with a hand rail, or a metal railing on a concrete kerb; lamp poles every 30 m; metal pedestrian fence; "ITO Bridge" signboards [E].
- **Wear.** 0.4: stained piers with a flood line, dried silt at the base, hanging garlands and scraps tied to the railing, fishing nets and sand piles below [E].
- **Barrage next door.** The ITO barrage downstream of the Wazirabad barrage is managed by Haryana; the Wazirabad barrage (1959) is a 1,491 ft weir with 6 under-sluices of 60 ft and 17 spillway bays of 58 ft [S: Wikipedia]. Barrage kit: a row of steel gates between grey concrete piers, a service deck, standing water on the upstream side.

## BRI-Y2 Signature Bridge (cable stay, later area)

- **Where.** Wazirabad to East Delhi, outside the baked pilot (28.71, 77.23). Opened 4 November 2018 [S: Wikipedia].
- **Structure.** Asymmetrical cantilever spar cable stayed bridge, 675 m long, 35.2 m wide, 2 x 4 lanes, main span 251 m, pylon 154 m viewing box (165 m above ground); steel pylon 5,800 t, steel deck 7,400 t; designed by Schlaich Bergermann Partner [S: Wikipedia]. Two inclined pylon columns bend mid-way; cables in a radial and semi-harp arrangement [S].
- **Approach ramps.** Curved concrete ramp deck on tall rectangular piers with flared tops, concrete barriers [P: flyover_signature_bridge_ramp.jpg].
- **Colour.** White and silver steel pylon and cables, grey deck, LED lighting at night.
- **Use.** Reference for a clean modern wear 0.1 bridge; not part of the pilot.

## RBR-U1 Rail bridges over roads (Northern Railway underbridges)

- **Where.** Minto Bridge, Tilak Bridge, Kashmere Gate and Mori Gate crossings, Pul Bangash and Sadar Bazar (OSM rail bridges, layer 1, about 31 unnamed ways plus the named Mumbai and Howrah line pieces) [S: OSM, bake].
- **Types.** (a) Masonry arch (Minto Bridge, 1926 to 1933 [S]); (b) steel plate girder or riveted girder deck on masonry or RCC abutments with a ballasted or open deck; (c) RCC box. Tilak Bridge station dates from 1924 and was first named Hardinge Bridge [S].
- **Look.** Black steel girders 1.2 to 1.8 m deep over a 2 lane road, 6 to 7 m clearance, ballast shoulders visible at the parapet, overhead line masts standing on the deck, a yellow clearance gauge board, pigeon netting, posters on the abutments [E].
- **Dimensions.** Road clearance 5.5 to 6.5 m, abutment span 10 to 14 m per track pair [E].

## BRI-ROB Road over rail (railway overbridge) and station flyovers

- **Where.** Mori Gate Road bridge (OSM way 77619341, 137 m tertiary road), New Delhi Railway Station Flyover (OSM 463710731, 704 m), the Rani Jhansi and Hanuman Mandir decks near the rail corridor [S: OSM].
- **Look.** FLY-T1 style (see flyovers.md), with a taller deck (7 m over rail, enough for the overhead line plus 1 m) and a solid parapet with fence screens against objects thrown on the line [E].
- **From the road deck.** Views down into the yard show blue platform roofs, overhead line masts and tracks [P: rail_new_delhi_platform_roofs.jpg, rail_delhi_junction_track_ohe.jpg].

## RBR-CUT Railway cut under road

- **Where.** OSM rail tunnels layer -1 near Kashmere Gate and Mori Gate (about 8 ways, 527 m, centre about (28.6605, 77.2169)) [S: OSM]. See rail.md for the track cross section.
- **Look.** Short cut and cover boxes with concrete portals, a black brick or concrete interior, a few lamps, ballast track continuing, overhead line with a lowered wire and a "low OHE" marker [E].

## BRI-MIN Small service bridges

- **Where.** OSM service bridges (15 m), drain crossings and gate bridges across the Najafgarh / Barapullah drains and canal remains [S: OSM].
- **Look.** Stone slab or RCC slab bridge, painted railing, nothing else [E].
