# Flyovers, ramps and elevated roads

Tags: [S] source, [P] read from a photo in img/, [E] estimate (see README.md). Written 2026-11-13.

Delhi has over 100 flyovers and bridges on PWD roads alone [S: pwddelhi.gov.in summary, 102+]. Two build eras show: the older junction flyovers (1980s to 2000s) and the newer precast box girder and elevated corridors (2000s on, Commonwealth Games 2010 and after). The metro viaduct is a separate family (`metro.md`).

Common to all: carriageways of 3.5 m lanes (IRC), clearance over roads at least 5.5 m (IRC:5, quoted in metro design basis documents [S: Noida Metro DBR snippet]), kerbs painted black and white, orange or grey crash rails, tall lamp poles, exposed drainage downpipes. The PWD inventory table lists name, length, number of lanes, vertical clearance and year [S: pwddelhi.gov.in flyover report columns; the data did not load in the fetch].

## FLY-T1 Older T beam or I girder flyover on twin or portal piers

- **Where in the pilot.** ITO Flyover and its ramps, Hanuman Mandir Flyover (Ring Road at Yamuna Bazar), New Delhi Railway Station Flyover (Ajmeri Gate to Paharganj, 2 lane over the yard), Shivaji Flyover and its paired deck (Maharaja Ranjeet Singh Marg), an unnamed tertiary flyover near Daryaganj, the short Ring Road bridge near Loha Pul. Eras are inferred, not confirmed: the older junction flyovers on trunk roads get T1, check each against a photo before building [E].
- **Same family outside the pilot.** AIIMS flyover, Ashram flyover, Dhaula Kuan, Moolchand.
- **Structure.** Continuous deck of precast or cast-in-place beams (T or I section) with a thin slab, simple spans of 20 to 30 m with expansion joints at each pier, piers either a pair of 1.2 to 1.5 m columns carrying a flared cap beam or a single stem with a wide flared cap (hammerhead) [P: flyover_aiims_ring_road.jpg shows a wide flat deck with a stepped pier cap and square-section piers] [E]. Deck depth 1.5 to 2 m, deck width 8.5 to 9.5 m for 2 lanes, 11.5 to 12 m for 3 lanes, two such decks side by side for a 4 to 6 lane flyover [E].
- **Barrier and railing.** The most typical Delhi sight: a 0.45 m kerb painted black and white every 0.5 m, with a tubular steel railing painted orange (about #e0661f) 1.1 m high on the edge, posts every 2 m; on ramps with an embankment, the railing runs along an RCC retaining wall [P: flyover_ashram.jpg, flyover_aiims_ring_road.jpg].
- **Colour.** Concrete grey #8c8a85 with a soot gradient from the deck edge down; piers stained black to 2 m; a yellow-black hazard band on nose walls; the underside of beams streaked with monsoon stains [P].
- **Lamps.** Single or double arm 10 to 12 m poles on the median or edge every 30 to 40 m, high mast 25 m at junction nodes, sodium amber or LED [P: AIIMS shows tall high-mast lamps].
- **Drainage and fittings.** Round downpipes (about 150 mm, grey or black) hugging the pier stem, scupper holes in the kerb, expansion joint strips with a rubber seal visible as a dark band across the deck every 25 m, concrete bearing pedestals [E].
- **Under the deck.** Often used: parked autos, tea stalls, small shrines, hoardings on the pier faces; keep it humane [E].
- **Kit.** Deck module 8.5, 9.5 and 11.5 m wide; pier twin-column and hammerhead; railing module (orange tube + black/white kerb); joint strip; lamp pole; downpipe.

## FLY-B1 Newer precast segmental or box girder flyover on single piers

- **Where in the pilot.** Sachivalaya Road Flyover (4 lane trunk, 1.9 km, 6 ways, near IP Estate and the Ring Road east, 28.635, 77.250) with its ramps, Geeta Colony Road Flyover (east bank), Rani Jhansi Flyover (Pul Bangash, just outside the baked outline) and the Delhi-Dehradun Expressway elevated pieces at the east edge. Treated as the "newer" family; verify era per structure [E].
- **Structure.** The precast segmental technique with rectangular box shaped elements was used for six lane Delhi flyovers [S: IABSE/ICJ snippet]; precast pretensioned girders were used on four Delhi flyover projects [S: same snippet]. Elevated corridors of 24.2 m with six lanes on a single narrow column have been built or planned in Delhi [S: Infrastructure Today snippet]. Single pier with a wide hammerhead cap, deck depth 1.8 to 2.4 m, spans 30 to 45 m [E].
- **Shape.** Smooth trapezoid or rectangular box soffit with cantilever wings, clean fascia, thin slab edge, no beam ribs. Piers slender rectangular, 1.6 to 2.5 m across, with a flared cap. Ramp deck in the Signature Bridge approach photograph: a curved concrete deck on tall rectangular piers with flared tops [P: flyover_signature_bridge_ramp.jpg].
- **Barrier.** Solid concrete crash barrier (New Jersey or F shape, 0.9 to 1.1 m high, 0.45 to 0.5 m wide) with a pipe railing or noise screen only on special sections; kerb paint black and white at the ends and approach walls; hazard yellow and black on nose walls [E].
- **Lights.** Poles on the barrier every 30 m; LED in the newer ones.
- **Wear.** 0.1 to 0.3: pale concrete, thin soot band under the wings, clean marks where hoardings were.
- **Kit.** Box deck module 9.5, 12, 18 and 24 m wide (a double carriageway on one box for the corridors), single pier with cap, barrier, light, joint.

## FLY-R1 Ramps, loops and approaches

- **Where.** Every flyover end and the junction loops: ITO Flyover Ramp (3 ways, single lane), Sachivalaya Road ramp (two ways, single lane), Hanuman Mandir link, Geeta Colony ramps (OSM `trunk_link`).
- **Structure.** Embankment ramps between retaining walls (RCC or reinforced earth panels, grey, with panel joints every 2 m) at about 1 in 30 to 1 in 25 grade, then the deck [E]. Loops are curved decks on single piers with a minimum radius 40 to 60 m [E].
- **Dressing.** Black-white painted kerb on the lower edge, orange railing along the top of the retaining walls, shrub planters at the base, drain inlet every 30 m, signage "Left turn / Ring Road" in blue-white [P: flyover_ashram.jpg shows the ramp, railing and painted kerb].

## FLY-E1 Elevated corridors on a single pier (Barapullah and later)

- **Where.** Barapullah Elevated Road (Sarai Kale Khan to INA, extended to Mayur Vihar over the Yamuna), outside the pilot (south of 28.604); a reference for the Ring Road corridor redevelopment plan [S: Wikipedia Ring Road, DPR 2025 to 2026].
- **Structure.** Precast segmental or in situ box on single piers; the Phase 3 river section is an extradosed bridge of about 550 m with a 128 m main span [S: single weak CEPT page]. Elevated road 4 lane or 6 lane, 17 to 25 m wide [E].
- **Kit.** Use the FLY-B1 box with a wider deck; do not apply to the pilot except if later tasks add elevated lanes along the Ring Road.
