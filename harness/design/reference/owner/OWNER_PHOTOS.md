## Pragati Maidan tunnel (P2-032)
- `pragati_maidan_tunnel/01_approach_ramp_mural_walls.jpg`: the open approach ramp down to the box mouth. Retaining walls painted end to end: cream base with pale grey flower discs, a blue and white river band, lotus, swans, trees in dark green; a thick saffron band along the top and a green band at the bottom (tricolour). Black and white striped kerbs, a narrow yellow-grid footway on one side, plain grey asphalt with white dashes. The mouth is a plain flat lintel with a small LED sign board over it.
- `pragati_maidan_tunnel/02_inside_wave_murals_marigold_ceiling.jpg`: inside the curved tunnel. Walls painted in big yellow and green wave bands with bird and leaf motifs; strings of orange marigold lights hang across the ceiling; two continuous white LED strip lines run along the ceiling edges; cable trays and pipes on the ceiling; black and white kerbs; fire point boxes in red on the wall.
- `pragati_maidan_tunnel/03_inside_painted_pillars_jet_fans.jpg`: the wide section with a central line of pillars. Each pillar painted in tall panels of different colours (saffron, purple, teal, yellow) with mandala and Madhubani style patterns; yellow and white striped plinths with small uplights at the base; big jet fans on the ceiling; long sign panels on the left wall; a grey concrete ceiling grid with marigold strings.
- `pragati_maidan_tunnel/04_entry_shed_signs_rain.webp`: street level near the tunnel and Pragati Maidan in rain. A long curved metal and polycarbonate shed (pedestrian cover) with a G20 sign; red and white plastic barriers and a yellow Delhi Police barricade; a blue gantry direction sign ("Pragati Maidan Parking", "Rajghat", heavy vehicles not allowed); red sandstone kerb wall and red and grey paver footpath with a yellow tactile strip; black and white kerbs; a big puddle reflecting everything; a soft drinks cart; scooters.

## GB Road (P2-024)
- `gb_road/02_street_front_signboards_day.webp`: GB Road (Shraddhanand Marg) at day. Old three and four storey buildings, crumbling plaster in faded yellow, ochre and grey, open balconies and wooden or iron screens on upper floors, the ground floor all hardware and machinery shops with dense, layered, faded sign boards; tarps on roofs; wires across; autos and cars in the street.
- `gb_road/03_night_hardware_shops_below.webp`: GB Road at night. Ground floor shutters down, big yellow and blue printed flex boards of machine parts shops ("Engineers and Traders", motors, pumps) with phone numbers and product pictures; upper floors decayed, with lattice (jali) balcony parapets, peeling paint, a dim coloured light in a window; dense tangled wires and poles; litter on the kerb; parked bikes and a cart.
- `gb_road/01_building_types_clothes_balconies.webp`, `04_upper_floors_and_street.avif`, `05_night_scene.avif`: chawl style buildings with clothes hanging from every window and balcony, people on the street and at doorways, women standing at ground floor doors (04, 05). NOTE: these look like Mumbai (Kamathipura) or Kolkata (Sonagachi) rather than GB Road (the window shutters, chawl corridors and the street are not GB Road's form). Use them for mood and for how upper floors live (clothes, shutters, lights), not for building shape. Respect rule (GAME.md): the people of this area are shown with dignity, never as a spectacle or a target; no explicit content.

## Chandni Chowk main road (P2-047, P2-044, P2-023)
- `chandni_chowk_main/01_sandstone_street_sisganj_day.jpg`: from above, at Gurudwara Sis Ganj. The whole street is red sandstone paving, wall to wall, with no asphalt carriageway and no kerb: one level surface. Rows of red sandstone bollards (squat, square, about 0.6 m) mark lanes; low square planters with green shrubs and nets down the middle; heritage double-arm black lamp posts with lantern heads; cycle rickshaws (red and blue seats), hand carts, people everywhere. Buildings: pink, cream and red sandstone fronts, Sis Ganj with gold domes.
- `chandni_chowk_main/02_night_lit_street.jpg`: the same street at night after rain: warm lamp light, the wet sandstone glowing orange, lit shop fronts, crowds, Sis Ganj floodlit in white and gold.
- `chandni_chowk_main/03_centre_line_planters_bollards.jpg`: standing on the centre line looking east to the Red Fort. Pale pink sandstone paving, a central row of round sandstone kerbed planter beds with green nets and big terracotta pots; carved sandstone bollards in lines; sandstone stalls and kiosks; shop houses with many stacked boards; overhead wires still cross; Gauri Shankar temple spires.
- `chandni_chowk_main/04_trilingual_sandstone_name_stone.jpg`: a carved red sandstone name stone reading "Chandni Chowk" in English, Hindi, Punjabi and Urdu, in front of Sis Ganj; palms and planters behind.
- `chandni_chowk_main/05_rickshaws_carved_bollards_red_fort.webp`: cycle rickshaws on the paving with the Red Fort behind. Close detail of the bollards: octagonal red sandstone posts with carved star patterns, terracotta pots, a red sandstone jali screen fence, a two storey red and white pavilion style building.

## Sign boards for old places (P2-048)
- `signboards/01_game_reference_street_of_painted_boards.png`: a screenshot from a big-budget shooter set in an Indian hill town (quality reference only; never copy its art or text). Shows the bar we want for boards: hand-painted tin and wood boards with uneven letters, a Hindi line over an English line, an arrow painted on the board, a tailor's board with a painted figure, a small laundry board, paper notices pasted on walls; chipped edges, rust streaks, dirt in the corners, paint fading unevenly; boards screwed to the wall or hung on the beam above the shutter, slightly tilted; a lamp on a bracket lighting a board.
- `signboards/02_game_reference_welcome_board_with_lamp.png`: the same game, a green "welcome" board with a two-line address under a bracket lamp on a wooden facade: a lamp placed to light a board, board on the facade above the door.
- `signboards/03_real_delhi_old_shop_signs_collage.png`: a collage of real Delhi and small-town shop signs (Hindustan Times, Sahapedia, The Wire, Alamy and Instagram results; reference only). Kinds: hand-painted boards with shadowed serif and script letters (book depot, film distributors, studio and photo shop with a painted face, tailors, a jeweller's "Ghantewala" board with an "estd" year and phone number), round enamel-style boards in Hindi ("purana sona chandi"), lettering painted straight onto shutters and doors, tall vertical boards running up building fronts (hotel and restaurant names), walls of stacked flex and tin boards in Paharganj and the old city (travel, STD, internet, money change, laminating), backlit acrylic boxes of newer shops, brand dealer boards (mobile shops). Colours: faded yellow, red, blue and white grounds, black and red letters with drop shadows; heavy wear, dust and rust.
