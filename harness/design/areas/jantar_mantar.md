## Footpaths

Inside the garden: red sandstone paving in long straight paths with a stone edge. Outside: Parliament Street footpath about 3 m, grey paver with a yellow-black kerb stripe. The footpath is public but often fenced for protests.

## Road surface and markings

Parliament Street / Sansad Marg: wide four-lane road with a central divider, grey asphalt in good condition, clear white lines and zebra crossings. Slightly better kept than a typical arterial because it is near Parliament.

## Street lamps

The garden has old black cast lanterns on stone pillars. The road has double-arm steel poles at about 9 m, 40 m spacing.

## Boundaries

Garden: low stone wall and a green iron railing around the observatory, an entrance gate with a red nameplate in Hindi and English. Street side: police barricades and wire fences where protests are held; this is the iconic protest road.

## Buildings

The observatory (1724): large geometric instruments in red-orange lime plaster with cream or white stone edges, stairs and arches. Colour: red-orange #c0502d-like, cream trim. Around it: government blocks (Akashvani Bhawan, post office, Reserve Bank towers) in 5 to 12 storeys, cream and brown, and hotel towers.

## Trees and planting

A formal garden: neat lawns, palms, neem, flowering shrubs and beds. Planting is tidy.

## Vendors and street life

Tourists and school groups inside, guards at the gate, ice cream and tea carts at the street, protesters and sit-ins at the roadside with banners and tents.

## Parking and vehicles

Police buses and vans on the road, official cars, few private cars. Auto-rickshaws at the gate.

## Typical clutter and wear

Wear 0.1. The monument is kept clean; the street is lightly dusty. Protest banners, tarps and tents are temporary clutter.

## What makes this area unlike the others

Giant red and cream astronomical instruments in a green park right beside a government road. Unmistakable silhouette. The street is the protest street of Delhi: barricades, tents and banners.

## Edge blending (what changes first)

Edges: it sits between CP (north) and Parliament / Lutyens (south). North: colonnade and commercial towers. South: low walls, avenue trees and sandstone government blocks. The first change is the vanishing of shop signs and the appearance of gates and guard boxes.
