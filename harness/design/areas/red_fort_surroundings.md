## Footpaths

On Netaji Subhash Marg: about 2.5 m of broken concrete or grey tile footpath beside a low retaining wall. At the Lahori Gate plaza: a wide open asphalt and stone forecourt with painted crowd lines.

## Road surface and markings

Wide arterial, 3 lanes each way, with a kerbed central divider and clear dashed lines. Grey and worn asphalt, patched. At the fort: open asphalt plaza with painted zones, no markings, yellow police barricades.

## Street lamps

Tall steel arm poles on the road, 9 to 10 m, single curved arms. Around the fort: modern floodlights at ground level lighting the red sandstone from below.

## Boundaries

The fort wall itself, 18 to 33 m high red sandstone with a dry moat; low railing along the road; police barricades; iron railing along the retaining wall. Security checkpoints at the gate.

## Buildings

Red Fort: red Agra sandstone walls with battlements, domed chhatris, the Lahori Gate with the flag and white marble top. Across the road: plain white 3 to 4 storey police and administrative blocks and Old Delhi shops. Colours: red sandstone #a4533b, white marble.

## Trees and planting

Trees on the road side (neem, gulmohar), clipped bushes, the green moat lawn between wall and road. A few big trees at the Subhash Park. Lawn is short grass.

## Vendors and street life

Tourists in lines at the gate, rickshaw pullers, vendors selling flags and snacks, ticket queues, guards. Independence Day crowds on 15 August. Pigeons.

## Parking and vehicles

Tour buses and autos in lines on the plaza, rickshaws waiting, police vans at the barricades. Not much private parking.

## Typical clutter and wear

Wear 0.35. Stone is dusty and stained, bird droppings, litter near the entrance. Plenty of barricades and signage boards in Hindi and English.

## What makes this area unlike the others

Monumental red wall as the backdrop to a street: a continuous 20 to 30 m red sandstone rampart with a single huge gate. No other area has red sandstone or a security-managed plaza.

## Edge blending (what changes first)

Edges: Lahori Gate plaza opens west to Chandni Chowk (the bazaar starts after about 80 m, wires and shop signs first); north to Netaji Subhash Marg and Subhash Park; the ring road to the east. First change: boards and wires appear, the white blocks give way to red-brown shops.
