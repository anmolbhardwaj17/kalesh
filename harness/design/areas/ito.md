## Footpaths

Wide pavements for an arterial, about 3 to 4 m, grey or red concrete paver, broken in places, with a foot-over skywalk of 535 m linking ITO and Pragati Maidan metro (see sources). Kerbs painted yellow and black. Pedestrians often walk in the road.

## Road surface and markings

Six to eight lanes at the crossing, asphalt of mixed age, thick white lane lines, stop lines and zebra crossings often faded. Flyovers carry Vikas Marg traffic over the junction; ramps with concrete jersey barriers, yellow and black kerb marks. Surface patched, oil-stained (heavy traffic).

## Street lamps

Tall highmast and curved-arm steel poles on the flyovers and the main road, sodium orange at night. Smaller double-arm poles on the side streets.

## Boundaries

Metal railing on the flyover, concrete parapets, jersey barriers, brick boundary walls of old bungalow-era blocks (Todarmal Lane, brick walls with green NDMC signs). Police barricades at the crossing.

## Buildings

Government and media offices: Delhi Police Headquarters glass and concrete slab, about 10 to 15 floors, Vikas Minar, Doordarshan Bhawan, newspaper offices on Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, Delhi High Court and Supreme Court area. Mixed 4 to 12 floor slab blocks, grey and brown concrete, tinted glass, AC boxes. Behind it the Purana Quila walls rise above trees. Metro stations are standard.

## Trees and planting

Large roadside trees (neem, peepal, banyan) along Safdar Hashmi Marg and Tilak Marg; thin planting on the flyover verges; bushes in the median.

## Vendors and street life

Heavy but anonymous traffic: office workers, journalists, tea stalls, juice carts, bus stops with crowds, auto-rickshaw queues. Fewer tourists. Night: tea stalls and snack carts near the metro.

## Parking and vehicles

Buses (DTC green), autos, taxis, delivery vans, cars. Informal roadside parking all the way along the dividers. Cycle and cart lanes are missing.

## Typical clutter and wear

Wear 0.4. Dust, soot on the underside of the flyover, bill posters, torn flex banners, hoardings, mud. Concrete grey-brown, rarely painted.

## What makes this area unlike the others

The only pilot area built around flyovers and a huge signal-free junction, with grey slab offices and the skywalk. The sense is infrastructure scale and noise, not heritage. Closest thing to modern Delhi traffic.

## Edge blending (what changes first)

Edges: to the west the Mandi House and Bengali Market side shifts to red-brick colonial-era blocks and cultural buildings; to the east the Yamuna bridge and Pragati Maidan with Purana Quila. First change: building age and colour (brick instead of concrete), lamps swap from highmast to double-arm.
