## Footpaths

Colonnade footpath on the building side is about 4 to 5 m of cream paving, as on the inner circle but with more broken sections. On the traffic side of the outer road the footpath is narrower, about 2 to 3 m, grey paver with kerb painted yellow and black. Subway stairs open straight onto the footpath.

## Road surface and markings

Wider than the inner ring: 3 to 4 lanes per direction on the outer circle in places, grey and black asphalt, white lane lines, yellow centre line on the radials, painted zebra crossings and stop lines. Road signs are green boards with white Hindi and English lettering. Surface wears faster and patches show.

## Street lamps

Same steel double-arm poles as the inner ring plus taller highmast lamps on the major junctions. Colours: pole grey or green, light sodium orange at night.

## Boundaries

Painted metal pedestrian railing at crossings and subway mouths, jersey barriers where works run, bus stop shelters. Plaza Building H block arcade has deep arched passages with white stuccoed walls.

## Buildings

Outer ring blocks: the same two storey colonnade, but the outer face is more commercial, with arched passages cut through to the inner ring (Plaza building). Beyond the outer ring: 8 to 20 storey glass and concrete towers on Barakhamba Road and Kasturba Gandhi Marg. Signage is heavier than the inner face.

## Trees and planting

Mature street trees in a continuous line on the outer circle. Planters at the corners. Trees are less ordered than Lutyens avenues.

## Vendors and street life

More traffic and less leisure: office commuters, delivery riders, auto-rickshaw and taxi stands, hawkers at subway mouths. Janpath subway is a bright tiled underpass.

## Parking and vehicles

Roadside parking in rows, taxis, autos, buses, a lot of two-wheelers. Parking is dense on the outer ring.

## Typical clutter and wear

Wear 0.2. Works hoardings, bus stops with adverts, wires at crossings, dust on signs. Billboards on roofs.

## What makes this area unlike the others

The outer ring faces the city: more road, taller towers, subway mouths and bus traffic. The inner ring faces the park. Unlike the inner circle it has real arterial scale and gantry signs.

## Edge blending (what changes first)

Out of the outer circle the radials run into Janpath, Kasturba Gandhi Marg, Barakhamba Road: the first change is the colonnade ending and the building fronts becoming 5 to 8 storey slab offices with plain plaster and glass; lamps and kerb stripes stay the same for two blocks.
