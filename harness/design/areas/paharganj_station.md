## Footpaths

Main Bazaar: no real footpath; shops spill onto 1 m of broken concrete or paver at the shopfront. Station side: a wide forecourt with grey granite tile and railings. DB Gupta Road: 2 m footpath with kerbs, uneven.

## Road surface and markings

Main Bazaar is about 7 to 8 m wide, patched asphalt or concrete, no lane lines. DB Gupta Road is a four-lane one-way road with painted dashes. Under the station flyover the road is a wide junction with faded lines.

## Street lamps

Bare bulbs and tube lights at shopfronts, a few street poles with sodium heads, neon from hotel signs and hoardings. Large lit hoardings on the roof of hotels.

## Boundaries

Roll-down shutters in many colours (blue shutters), iron gates, narrow arch entrances for old lanes. Metal railings at the station. Walls of old havelis with brackets.

## Buildings

Dense, tall 4 to 6 storey buildings jammed edge to edge, painted pink, blue, yellow; large hotel and guesthouse signs stacked on every floor, rooftop terraces with cell masts, balconies, dish antennas and a rooftop mosque or temple. Old haveli fragments with carved brackets. Backpacker signs in English and Hebrew. The station: a long brown and cream concourse with a flyover and metal canopy.

## Trees and planting

Very little: a few peepal or neem at corners and roofs, potted plants on terraces. The station has a few trees.

## Vendors and street life

Backpackers, shopkeepers, porters, rickshaw pullers, fruit sellers, tailor shops, Tibetan monks, cows in the lane, tourists. Day and night market.

## Parking and vehicles

Cycle and e-rickshaws, motorbikes, autos, handcarts; cars at the station side; tour vans and taxis queue at the Ajmeri Gate exit. Buses at the flyover.

## Typical clutter and wear

Wear 0.8. Wires, garlands, tarpaulins, stained plaster, hanging signs, litter, cows, burnt oil, graffiti.

## What makes this area unlike the others

The backpacker bazaar and rail hub: international signs, hotel stacks, a station next door, trains behind the roofs, tourists mixed with locals, bright hoardings. No other pilot area has a rail terminal or foreign-language signs.

## Edge blending (what changes first)

Edges: east side meets the station and Ajmeri Gate; north-east joins the Chandni Chowk / Old Delhi side via Asaf Ali Road; south meets Karol Bagh and Connaught Place. First change: hotel stacks reduce to 3 to 4 floor shops and the bazaar widens, then big hoardings go.
