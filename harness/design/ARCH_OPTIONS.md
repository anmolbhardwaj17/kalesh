## How to read the tables

- **Measured (file):** the number comes from that bench report in `harness/reports/arch/` (or the named file). Those reports are not in git, so the numbers are copied here.
- **Est:** an estimate. "Est kept" means the bench did not measure it.
- **Computed:** worked out in this task from measured or drafted inputs (for example bearings from `harness/data/districts.json` with `tools/geo/projection.ts`). Not a bake.
- **Wrong estimate:** what draft 1 guessed and how it was wrong. "Estimate held" means the guess was right.
- Sizes that define an option (a 250 m tile, a 25,000 triangle limit) are the option itself, not a measurement.
- Complexity is an estimate in builder tasks after the base exists: S under 2, M 2 to 3, L 3 to 5, XL more than 5.
- Every option has an id (R1, P2 ...). The recommendation and the sign-off quote use the ids. **Rec** marks the recommendation after the benches; "changed" means it differs from draft 1.
- The Bench column names the task and the report. "not measured" rows keep their estimate.

## What was known before the benches

Measured in P0-009 (`harness/look_proof/baseline_vulkan/perf.json`, `harness/look_proof/baseline/perf.json`): the untextured stand-in street, landmarks and area kits, 9 views. GPU p95 1.5 to 3.2 ms over two Vulkan runs, CPU p95 under 0.4 ms, 84 to 175 draw calls, 0.79 to 1.95 M triangles drawn with shadow passes, 192 MB video memory in the busiest view, load 0.56 to 0.82 s.

Measured on the pinned engine in P0-010 (a headless read of ClassDB and ProjectSettings):
- The build is single precision (`OS.has_feature("double")` is false).
- Physics engines offered: DEFAULT, Jolt Physics, GodotPhysics3D, Dummy. The project is on DEFAULT, which resolves to GodotPhysics3D here, measured (physics.json).
- Rendering methods: forward_plus, mobile, gl_compatibility. Drivers: Metal or Vulkan on macOS, Vulkan or D3D12 on Windows.
- Upscalers: bilinear, FSR 1.0, FSR 2.2, and MetalFX spatial and temporal in the viewport API.
- `PhysicsServer3D` has `set_active` but no manual space step, so game code cannot step physics by hand.
- Present: MultiMesh, visibility ranges on GeometryInstance3D, OccluderInstance3D, LightmapGI, VoxelGI, NavigationServer3D with async baking, PhysicalBoneSimulator3D, RetargetModifier3D, the IK modifiers, ResourceLoader threaded loads, WorkerThreadPool, GLTFDocument, `Image.load_ktx_from_buffer`, mesh LODs through `ArrayMesh.add_surface_from_arrays`.
- Absent: any built-in terrain, world partition, tile streaming or HLOD builder. We build those.

Toolchain on this Mac: Node 22.22, Python 3.14 (Homebrew), clang, Blender, ffmpeg, the osmium command line tool, Xcode (beta). Not present: scons, cmake, cargo, GDAL. 144 GB of free disk (measured in P0-010).

Map size: draft 1 estimated layout C as islands (map box 10.1 x 8.9 km, detailed bake 31.7 km2, about 510 tiles at 250 m; pilot about 7.2 km2). The seamless map is bigger: about 68 km2 and about 1,090 tiles for the first release, and about 20 km2 for the pilot centre (estimates, "District layout recommendation").

Top speeds (decisions.md "Speeds and map size", GAME.md): auto 65 km/h, car 140, fast car 180 to 200. At 200 km/h a 250 m tile passes in 4.5 s (computed).

### The plan

1. **Sources (measured choices).** Roads: OSM only (RS2). Buildings: Overture (BS1), with landmarks joined on OSM directly because Overture drops the wikidata tag. Ground: GEDTM30 smoothed over 60 m (GS1), filtered GLO-30 as the fallback. Every file is pinned by date and checksum in `data/SOURCES.json` (16 files pinned, none unreachable, measured).
2. **Rule engine (RE1).** The eight benched rules stay. Added before the pilot bake: the fit rules that the road prototype needed (R-LANE-FIT: shift a carriageway that is drawn closer than its lanes are wide, redraw a slip road that is drawn as a short diagonal, take a lane count from the road ahead), whole-street lane inference (the 4 taper findings at ITO), and the metro, foot bridge, stitch, hero and remaining terrain rules of the catalogue.
3. **Auto-repair bounds (AR1), confirmed.** A repair applies on its own at confidence 0.8 or more and a low impact. Low impact means: a join of 5 m or less; a missing bridge layer tag; a building reaching 1.5 m or less into a class-default road width (the width gives way, the building stays); an exact duplicate footprint; a height outlier of 3 m or less; a sideways carriageway shift of 2.5 m or less (the bench needed 2.19 m at most). Never automatic: anything on a hero road, a one-way with no pair, a slip road redraw over 2.5 m (the bench needed up to 9.17 m), a change of lane count on a trunk or primary road.
4. **A queued issue never blocks a bake.** The top proposal is applied as provisional, drawn in the game, and listed in the inspector. Anmol's pick replaces it through an override. This keeps the never-wait rule.
5. **Overrides (OV1).** One patch file per world tile, found by id and then by anchor (measured: both ways work, a stale patch is kept and reported).
6. **Lane model, heights, junctions, ground (LN1, EL1, J3, TF2).** Proven on ITO and CP with 0 overlap, 0 continuity breaks, 0 height steps and a 0 m road to ground gap (measured, roads.json). Production list: a sparse height solver, a constrained Delaunay ground, tiles cut at 250 m, lane UVs with markings drawn in the road shader (markings are 28% of the ITO triangles), deck joints bridged in the collider, real supports, and one road graph per continuous block.
7. **Health report and gate counts.** `harness/reports/map/health.json` per bake. Gate counts are zero or explained: open ports, floating ends, overlapping road faces, lane continuity breaks, tapers out of limits. Measured today: all 0 at ITO and CP except 4 explained tapers.

## District layout recommendation

### What the stitch bench showed (measured, stitch.json)

The stitching mechanics pass: 0 open ports of 172, landmark error 0.006 mm, no rotation or mirror in 4 placements, three authored connectors with 0 grade, radius or clearance violations, 138 of 172 ports (80.2%) closed by rule, bake 2.4 s. But the bench built districts as islands: four frames in a 6 by 5.25 km box, at least 803.6 m apart, with connectors 2.1 to 2.9 km long over open stand-in ground. Filler blocks were not built.

### Layout rules (new, checked by the bake as part of R-STITCH)

1. **North up, true scale, no rotation, no mirror** inside every real area. Measured true today.
2. **The centre is one continuous block at factor 1.0.** Every real place in it sits at its real position relative to India Gate. So every pair in the centre keeps its real bearing exactly and its real distance. Gate: every pair within 0.5 degrees and 0.5% (far inside the 5 degree rule).
3. **One frame and one road graph per continuous block.** No ports and no connectors inside a block: streets simply run on. Two frames would disagree by up to 0.31 m at shared streets (measured, stitch.json).
4. **Far districts sit on their real bearing from India Gate, all at the same factor.** So they are exact as seen from the middle of the map, and exact among themselves.
5. **No bare ground** anywhere inside the map outline. Every tile is real city, generated city, real open land drawn as the real place, or water.
6. **One world tile grid.** Tiles ignore district outlines, and area profiles blend across every edge (EB1), so no border can show.

### Why the centre factor is 1.0 (computed)

- The pilot districts are close in reality. Gaps between the pilot download boxes: Connaught Place to India Gate 0.44 km, to Chandni Chowk 0.83 km, to ITO 1.03 km; ITO to Chandni Chowk 1.22 km; India Gate and ITO touch (computed from the boxes in pilot_data.json).
- The smallest even factor at which the four pilot clips, as drafted, do not overlap is 0.68. With a 200 m seam between them it is 0.79, with 400 m it is 0.90. Connaught Place and India Gate are the tight pair.
- The areas Anmol wants in between (Paharganj, Daryaganj, Mandi House, Pragati Maidan, Karol Bagh) are real places that need their real room. Paharganj, GB Road and Chandni Chowk already touch as drafted, so with them in, no factor under 1.0 fits.
- So the honest even factor for the centre is 1.0: stop cutting islands and bake the centre as one real piece.

### Corrected offsets, and the method

Method (for `tools/bake/stitch/`, replacing the hand-set offsets of `stitch_draft.mjs`):

1. Pick the pivot: India Gate (28.612894, 77.229446), the origin of the centre block's frame.
2. For a place in the centre block: world position = pivot world position + `geoToWorld(lat, lon, pivot)`. No per-district offset exists any more inside the block.
3. For a far district with its own frame origin: offset = pivot world position + factor x `geoToWorld(origin, pivot)`. One factor for all far districts.
4. Move the world origin to the centre of the final map box (subtract one vector from everything), so no point is more than about 7 km out.
5. The bake prints the pair table (real against game bearing and distance for every pair) into the health report and fails if a centre pair is off by more than 0.5 degrees.

## Recommendation

One line per decision, after the benches. "Changed" means it differs from draft 1.

- **Renderer: R1 Forward+.** 7.06 ms for the bench city under stylised B. Mobile is cheaper but loses SSAO and SSIL, which the look needs.
- **Graphics driver: G1.** Metal for players (6.55 against 6.82 ms), Vulkan here only to read GPU times.
- **Lighting: L1.** 3.55 ms for the whole look, no lag. SDFGI adds 16.6 ms at speed and is out.
- **Anti-aliasing and scaling: A4 (changed).** Draw at 1080p, stretch with bilinear, TAA as a switch. The temporal upscalers cost more than they save here.
- **Physics engine: P1 Jolt, set by name.** Same step time as the other engine, better rays and ragdolls.
- **Sim clock: C1.** Proven: replays and holds are exact.
- **Vehicle: V2.** Same numbers as VehicleBody3D on the old tune, plus one-tick response, per-axle grip and a working three wheeler, at 0.035 ms per car.
- **On foot: F1.** Passes kerbs, stairs and ramps at the design speeds.
- **Colliders: K3 in chunks (changed in detail).** Exact where you drive and walk, 1.4 MB per tile, attached in pieces of about 500 triangles.
- **Game language: LANG1 GDScript.** 230 agents think in 0.8 ms. No C++ needed.
- **Bake language: B1 Node and TypeScript.** Every source was read without Python.
- **Tile format: T1.** Smallest attach cost, 1 ms read, one file for the game and the inspector.
- **Coordinates: X1 as block frames (changed).** One frame per continuous block, own frames for far districts.
- **Float precision: FP1.** No shimmer or jitter out to 7 km.
- **Map layout: MC** for the district list and stages (owner's pick), placed by **CG2 (new):** a continuous centre at factor 1.0, far districts on their real bearing at one factor (question 4).
- **District outlines: DC2 (changed),** with the DC3 band only on the outer edge and along corridors.
- **Connectors: SC1,** for corridors and bridges only. Shortened real roads fail below 0.45.
- **Filler: FL4 (changed, owner decision).** No bare ground: real in-between areas, generated city blended from the neighbours, real open land drawn as itself.
- **Tile size: ST1,** on one world grid. 250 m costs the same in total as 500 m and loads four times finer.
- **Tile loading: TL1,** with a 1 ms attach cap per frame.
- **Instancing: I3 with LD1.** Together 7.06 ms and 75 MB of buffers. Grime wear travels in the MultiMesh custom data.
- **LOD: LD1.** Cuts 92% of the triangles. The cross-fade still has to be measured.
- **Occlusion culling: OC2 none (changed).** With LOD it saves 0.2 ms and costs 0.4.
- **Textures: TX1.** 7 times less memory, both import sets.
- **Navigation: N1.** Not benched; chosen on reasoning. N2 stays the fallback.
- **Crowds: AN2.** 300 people for 0.32 ms CPU and 0.88 ms GPU. Re-measure with the real rig.
- **Animation library: AL1.** Owner's answer.
- **Combat: CB1.** Rays 0.03 ms, ragdolls 0.06 ms each.
- **Traffic: TR1.** 100 physics cars would cost 3.5 ms.
- **Road source: RS2 OSM only (changed).** Overture's roads are OSM.
- **Building source: BS1 Overture.** 15 times the footprints of OSM alone in Chandni Chowk, and far fewer buildings on roads than our own merge.
- **Ground height: GS1, smoothed over 60 m (changed in detail).** Raw it fails. Filtered GLO-30 is the fallback.
- **Rule engine: RE1.** Eight rules run in under 60 ms per district.
- **Auto-repair: AR1,** with the bounds listed in the plan above. 73% automatic.
- **Overrides: OV1.** Found by id and by anchor.
- **Elevation solver: EL1.** 0 steps, 0 grade and clearance violations at ITO.
- **Lane model: LN1,** with the fit step as logged repairs.
- **Hero roads: HR1.** The CP inner circle spec bakes with 0 mismatches.
- **Junction builder: J3.** 0 m2 of overlap. The polygon union part waits for question 1.
- **Terrain: TF2.** 0 m gap against 0.50 m for the grid.
- **Map inspector: MI1, kept small.** Four steps, issues first.
- **Inspector views: IV1 in the order of IV3.**
- **Landmark matching: LM1.** 351 pilot candidates; the 50 m check becomes "inside the footprint, or within 50 m".
- **Landmark storage: LS2. Placement: LP2** (0.006 mm error). **Hero models: HM2.**
- **Area division: AD2,** with archetype profiles by rule for in-between areas first.
- **Profile storage: PS1. Area generator: AG1** (0.57 MB per dense tile). **Kits: KT2. Edge blending: EB1,** which is what makes districts blend.
- **Verify harness: VH2.** The benches ran on it.
- **Mac packaging: EM1 for now** (owner deferred signing). **Windows: EW1,** tested on his PC and a friend's from the first playable.
- **Cutscenes: CS2.** Data files and one player, so scenes are text that can be tested.
- **Lip sync: LIP2 in the slice and in play, LIP1 for big cutscenes once voices exist.**
- **Faces: FC2 on the leads and named cast, from the first big cutscene.**
- **Dialogue: DG1,** our own line player with channels and interruption.
- **Subtitles: SB1,** Hinglish and English from the start (question 5).
- **Skip and resume: SK1,** every cutscene declares its end state.
