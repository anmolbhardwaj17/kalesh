# Kalesh: story seed (orchestrator draft, 2026-11-10)

Starting point for the story outline (task P0-032). Anmol: "The game should look banger, the story should be banger, no compromises." Everything here is a proposal for him to tear apart. Names are working names.

## Hook
One night at Connaught Place a passenger leaves a bag in Bittu's auto: cash and a diary of fifteen years of payments to the people who run Delhi. Everyone wants it back. Three pages are missing.

## People
- **Bittu (driver), 28, Laxmi Nagar.** Pays off a rented auto, funny, never picks a side. His father died in a Chandni Chowk factory fire when he was 13. Arc: from "not my problem" to choosing to fight.
- **SI Meera Rana (cop).** Honest at a station where honesty is a career risk. ACP Khanna, a father figure, got her the posting. Arc: the system she believes in is the crime; burn it or keep the badge.
- **Danny / Dhananjay (fixer), Paharganj.** Charming, expensive. Fifteen years ago, as a boy, he locked a door when told to: the factory where Bittu's father died. Never told anyone. Arc: guilt dressed up as cool.
- **Harish Sethi (the face).** Beloved philanthropist, hoardings, hospital trusts, entering politics with an invented party. The fire made him rich (insurance, then the land).
- **ACP Khanna.** Meera's mentor. He is in the diary. Players love him in Act 1.
- **"M".** The last name in the diary is only a signature. Never confirmed.

## Five acts (about 65 to 70 story missions, merged missions at each act's peak)
1. **Sawari** (CP, ITO, New Delhi station, Chandni Chowk, Red Fort): the bag; the passenger dies; Meera has Bittu's auto number; Danny is hired to get it back; all three circle each other; three-way chase through Chandni Chowk lanes to Red Fort where they meet. Ends on a call: "Gurugram mein milte hain."
2. **Paisa** (Gurugram towers, Cyber City, Laxmi Nagar, Kashmere Gate ISBT, Delhi Gymkhana): follow the money; Bittu's family threatened; a forced alliance; Gurugram tower heist; Sethi at a charity gala.
3. **Gaddari** (Vasant Kunj, Khan Market, Nizamuddin, airport): Khanna's betrayal; the fake encounter order; Bittu's brother arrested; airport runway chase, someone gets on a plane, someone does not.
4. **Jalaawatan** (Yamuna floodplains, Shahdara, Noida, Malcha Mahal): exile in a floodplain basti marked for demolition by Sethi's "world-class Delhi" project; Danny's playable flashback of the fire night; the woman at Malcha Mahal; Bittu learns about Danny.
5. **Kalesh** (core Delhi transformed): election result night, Sethi about to win; Lutyens sealed, CP crowds, India Gate lit; finale across Kartavya Path, Chandni Chowk rooftops, the Red Fort ramparts at dawn, 6:10, the train time from mission 1.

## Choices people will argue about
- The fake encounter (Act 3): Meera is ordered to "handle" a cuffed suspect. Shoot: career rises, conscience rots, the story remembers. Refuse: suspended, hunted by her own force.
- The Danny reveal (Act 4): forgive or not, in a quiet scene; changes Danny for the rest of the game.
- Sethi built the hospital that saved Bittu's mother. Was he all bad? Never fully answered.
- Endings: Expose (Meera's way: truth out, city erupts, her life over); Sell (Danny's way: rich, free, Bittu never speaks to him again); Burn (Bittu's way: Sethi falls quietly, system survives, family safe). Hidden fourth: all three missing pages open the last page.

## Mysteries
1. Who is "M"? Clues point to Khanna, the radio host, Meera's mother, an unseen minister. Never confirmed.
2. Three missing pages hidden across the city (an Old Delhi book stall, a Gurugram parking basement, Malcha Mahal); each reveals a name and a new question.
3. The woman at Malcha Mahal: ghost, survivor, or Bittu's mother's sister? Appears only at night, only to one of the three.
4. The late-night RJ on the auto radio knows things before the news. Post-credits: he says "6:10" and laughs.
5. 6:10 everywhere: clocks, train boards, graffiti.
6. The first passenger's name differs in every in-game newspaper.

## Controversy, from systems not communities
Demolitions for a "world-class city"; fake encounters and TV media trials; charity as a laundromat; gig workers and migrants who keep the city running and get blamed; an election with invented parties and politicians. Inside the respect rule: no real people or parties, no religion or caste as villain, no cruelty for fun.

## Cost note
About 3,000 dialogue lines and 20+ voiced characters. Writing is cheap; voice recording is the long pole, planned later.
