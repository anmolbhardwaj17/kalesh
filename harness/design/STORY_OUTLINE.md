## 1. Logline and theme

**Logline.** One night in Connaught Place, a frightened accountant leaves a red ledger in an auto. Inside: fifteen years of payments to the people who run Delhi, starting with a factory fire that killed fourteen workers. The auto driver whose father died in that fire, the honest cop whose father was ruined for telling the truth about it, and the fixer who locked the factory door as a boy are pulled into one hunt. Everyone wants the ledger back. Three pages are missing.

**Theme: who owns Delhi?** Not the people on the hoardings. Not the people who sweep, drive and deliver. The game's answer, act by act:
1. Sawari: nobody owns anything. Everyone carries something they did not pack.
2. Paisa: money owns Delhi. Charity, towers, GB Road, gig apps: it all flows to the same ledger.
3. Gaddari: the people you trust own you.
4. Jalaawatan: the city belongs to the people it pushes to the edges, and they know it.
5. Kalesh: Delhi is owned by whoever writes things down. The fight is over the record.

**Underneath:** one fire, three sons and a daughter of it. Bittu lost his father to it. Meera lost her father to the cover-up. Danny lost himself to it. None of them knows the others are part of the same night until Act 4.

**The fire (backstory, never shown in full until M42).** Fifteen years ago, 6 October, a garment godown in Kucha Gulab Rai, Chandni Chowk. An export order, workers kept inside overnight, the door locked from outside. The fire was set for insurance. It was reported at 6:10 am. Fourteen died; thirteen bodies were named. The case was closed as "short circuit". The land became Harish Sethi's first fortune.

**The ledger.** A red cloth-bound bahi khata, the kind every Old Delhi shop keeps. Fifteen years of entries in ink, some in an old traders' shorthand only old munshis read. Each payment has an amount, a date and initials. The last entry is only a signature: "M".

## 2. The villain and the plan, layer by layer

### Layer 1: Harish Sethi, the face
- 58. "Sethi-ji". Hoardings across the city: "Dilli Ka Dil". Runs the Sethi Seva Trust (hospitals, scholarships, langars at festivals). Standing for Chief Minister with the invented Nav Dilli Dal.
- His plan: **Dilli 2040**, a "world-class Delhi". A Yamuna riverfront that needs the floodplain bastis gone, and a "heritage redevelopment" of the Chandni Chowk block where the fire was. He has bought the land through trusts for years.
- What he did: paid for the fire. What he does not know, or says he does not: that people were inside. He built the first Seva hospital a year later. It saved thousands, including Bittu's mother. It also launders money: every free surgery is billed three times to a government scheme.
- His line: "Main Dilli ka maalik nahi hoon. Kiraayedar hoon." ("I don't own Delhi. I rent it.") Said in M65; players argue whether it is a confession or a lie.
- Is he all bad? Never fully answered. The hospital is real. The fire is real.

### Layer 2: ACP Mohan Khanna, the shield
- 55. Meera's mentor. Warm, funny, remembers birthdays, pays for chai. Players love him in Act 1.
- Fifteen years ago, as an inspector, he closed the fire case. He replaced Head Constable Balwant Rana's first report (the door was locked from outside) with "short circuit", then had Rana dismissed for a planted bribe. Rana died broke two years later. Khanna then paid Meera's school fees and got her this posting. Guilt, or a leash, or both.
- In the ledger as "K", monthly, for fifteen years.
- He ordered the accountant's murder (done by Gullu, M03's suspect) to protect himself, not Sethi. Sethi never asked for it. This is why Khanna wants the ledger more than Sethi does: page 1 holds his signature on the fire report.
- His end line (M61): "Main bhi bas ek panna tha, beta." ("I was just one page too.")

### Layer 3: the muscle
- **Bhola Pehalwan**, Sethi's head of security. Ex-wrestler, quiet, polite until he is not. Fifteen years ago he was the man who took the package from boy Danny and told him to lock the door. He set the fire. Wants Sethi's chair once Sethi is CM.
- **Inspector Dev Rawat**, Khanna's encounter man. Hunts Meera from Act 3.
- **Gullu**, Bhola's driver. Killed the accountant on Khanna's order. Meera arrests him in M03; Khanna frees him in M09; he is the cuffed man in the encounter (M32).

### Layer 4: "M", kept ambiguous on purpose
The last entry in the ledger is a signature. The same "M" approved the land conversion after the fire and the Dilli 2040 map. Someone also hired Danny through Jaggi to get the bag before Sethi did: a woman's voice on the phone, only ever "Madam". Is Madam M? Is Madam working for M? Never confirmed. Planted theories, each with real clues (full map in section 9):
- **Khanna** (Mohan): his pen, his access, his denial.
- **Malti Rana**, Meera's mother: her stamp on the land papers.
- **RJ Munna** of Raat Wali 106.1: he knows things before the news.
- **Mathur sahab**, an unseen minister: Sethi calls him "M-sahab" once.
- **The Malcha Mahal woman**, maybe Shanti, the fourteenth victim nobody identified: a woman's voice, old banknotes, and a "Shukriya" at the end.
- A fan theory we plant but never feed: M is Meera herself, in the future (section 9, the "M.R." page).

The game's honest answer to "who is M": the system needs an M. Whoever it is, there will be another one. The fourth ending makes that point and still never shows a face.

## 3. The three lives

### Bittu (driver), 28, Laxmi Nagar
Official name Vikas Kumar; nobody uses it. Rents his auto from Kalra sahab, owes eleven months. Funny, quick, talks to passengers, never picks a side. Wears his father's steel watch, stopped at 6:10, never repaired.

| | |
|---|---|
| Want | Own the auto. Keep Amma healthy, Sonu out of trouble. Stay out of it. |
| Need | To pick a side and say his father's name out loud. |
| Flaw | Jokes his way out of every hard moment. "Chhodo yaar." |
| Wound | Took his father's lunch tiffin to the factory the morning of the fire. Got there at 6:10. |
| Lie he believes | "People like us don't fight people like them. We drive them." |
| Change | From driving other people's stories to steering his own. In Act 5 he leads 500 autos into a sealed city. |
| Inner voice | Jokes, Hinglish, deflection. When it goes quiet, something is wrong. |

Ending by ending:
- **Expose:** Sonu cleared, Bittu on TV for a week, then back to fares. Sethi's hospital shuts; Amma's treatment stops. He drives Meera to court.
- **Sell:** Owns his auto and a flat in Dwarka. Never speaks to Danny again. Drives past Sethi's CM hoarding every day.
- **Burn:** Burns the ledger, keeps his father's page. Sethi withdraws. The basti gets a stay. Truth buried; he can live with it, mostly.
- **Aakhri Panna (hidden):** Drives Danny to the police station at sunrise with Meera in the back. The last fare of the game. No meter.

### SI Meera Rana (cop), 31
Sub-inspector, Rajdhani Police, Indraprastha thana near ITO. Clean, sharp, a little proud of being clean. Ties her hair the same way every shift. Khanna got her the posting.

| | |
|---|---|
| Want | To rise clean and prove the badge works. |
| Need | To see that the system she trusts is the crime, and to choose people over the badge. |
| Flaw | Faith in rules. Pride. She judges fast. |
| Wound | Her father, HC Balwant Rana, was dismissed for bribery when she was 16. She has been ashamed of him for fifteen years. |
| Lie she believes | "Bad cops are bad apples. My father was one. I am not him." |
| Change | Learns her father was the one honest man at the fire. Decides what the uniform means without anyone's permission. |
| Inner voice | Procedure. Lists. "Suspect, male, thirty. Car, stolen. Me, alone." Breaks into feeling only rarely. |

Ending by ending:
- **Expose:** The truth goes out. If she shot Gullu, the ledger holds her encounter fee too: she exposes herself and goes to jail. If she refused, Gullu testifies; she is free, without a badge.
- **Sell:** Keeps the badge. Transferred far from Delhi. In some playthroughs a new ledger page opens with "M.R." (section 9).
- **Burn:** Keeps the badge, stays inside, fights small fights. Khanna's replacement shakes her hand.
- **Aakhri Panna:** Arrests Danny herself on the rampart, by the book, and keeps the uniform: "Ab yeh vardi meri hai."

### Danny / Dhananjay (fixer), 28, Paharganj
Raised by Jaggi Chacha, an Old Delhi fixer, after his mother left. Charming, expensive, maroon jacket, always has a price. Rule: never ask what is inside. Keeps a dented steel tiffin on a shelf and never opens it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Want | Money, then Dubai. A life where nobody knows him. |
| Need | To ask what is inside, and to say what he did. |
| Flaw | Charm as a wall. Everything is a deal. |
| Wound | At 13 he carried a package to a godown and locked its door when told to. He heard knocking. He ran. |
| Lie he believes | "I was a kid. It's not mine to carry. Everything has a price, even this." |
| Change | Stops pricing things. Opens the tiffin. Confesses, or does not, by the player's road. |
| Inner voice | Prices. "Two lakh. High risk. Fine." The price goes quiet near Bittu. |

Ending by ending:
- **Expose:** The ledger names "J. ka ladka". Arrested, or runs and becomes a rumour, depending on the forgive choice.
- **Sell:** Rich and alone in Dubai. The last shot: he opens the tiffin, finally. We do not see inside.
- **Burn:** Leaves Delhi on the 6:10 train. Callback to M01.
- **Aakhri Panna:** Confesses on air at 6:10, names himself first. Arrested. Rides to the thana in Bittu's auto.

**The tiffin.** In M06 (the flashback) a boy bumps into young Danny in the Chowk; Danny catches the boy's tiffin. The boy is Bittu, taking lunch to his father. Danny never gave it back. Players will not notice in Act 1. In Act 4 the tiffin has "Ramesh" scratched on the lid.

## 5. How the story is told

Owner direction (2026-11-10): "I hope there will be cutscenes as well in this game to blend the story properly. Thinking, talking, dialogue."

Story moment types, used as tags in every mission below:
- **CUTSCENE:** a directed in-engine scene with camera cuts. Openings, reveals, act endings.
- **DRIVE TALK:** dialogue while the player drives, GTA style. Most of the story lives here.
- **PHONE:** calls and texts, in play or on the phone screen.
- **INNER:** the lead's inner voice. Short. Used sparingly.
- **RADIO:** RJ Munna on Raat Wali 106.1, or news bulletins.
- **AMBIENT:** overheard lines: crowds, TVs in shops, police scanners.

Rules:
1. **Cutscenes are short and skippable.** Most under 60 seconds, the big ones under 2 minutes. Skip is one button, always. A skipped scene leaves a one-line recap in the pause menu.
2. **Most talk happens in play.** Drives, walks, tails, stakeouts. If a scene can be a drive, it is a drive. The player keeps the wheel.
3. **Each act has 3 to 5 big directed cutscenes** (listed per act as "Big cutscenes"). Everything else is a short CUTSCENE beat (under 30 s) or play.
4. **Inner voice is a signature of the three leads.** Only the lead has it. Bittu jokes, Meera lists, Danny prices. One to three INNER lines per mission at most. Silence of the inner voice is a tool: in the big reveals it says nothing.
5. **Drive talk pauses for action.** If the player crashes or a chase starts, the line stops and resumes with a short "haan, toh..." pickup.
6. **The radio is a character.** RJ Munna can be switched off. People who leave it on hear clues first.
7. **Lines stay short:** under 15 words as a rule, never over 25.

### Act 1: Sawari (10 missions)

**Theme:** not my problem. Everyone carries something they did not pack.
**Places:** CP, Janpath, Barakhamba Road, ITO, New Delhi station forecourt, Chandni Chowk, Red Fort.
**Time:** one long day and two nights.
**Opening image:** close on Bittu's wrist. The steel watch says 6:10. It is 3 pm. Heat haze over a Chandni Chowk auto stand. He taps the watch, it does not move, he smiles anyway.
**Big cutscenes (4):** the bag left behind (M01); Danny's first favour opening (M06); the first meeting of the three (M07); the Red Fort stand-off and the call (M10).

**M01 Train at 6:10 (Driver).** Pick up a family at the Chowk, beat the clock to New Delhi station. The train leaves at 6:10. Bittu: "Bad time." Nobody asks why. At the station a nervous man with no luggage hails him: "CP. Jaldi." He gets out at the outer circle without paying, leaving a red ledger and cash in a bag under the seat.
- CUTSCENE: the opening at the stand (20 s). The munshi getting out, looking back once (15 s).
- DRIVE TALK: the family. Dadi stares at Bittu's watch. The father argues about the shortcut.
- INNER: "Train 6:10 pe. Of course."
- RADIO: RJ Munna's first words: "Dilli, aaj raat koi kuch bhool ke jaayega. Lautana mat."
- AMBIENT: a stand driver jokes about Bittu's broken watch.

**M02 Who wants the bag (Driver).** Same night. Two men in a dark car chase the auto around the CP circles. A police jeep joins; SI Meera Rana is at the wheel for a few seconds and reads the number plate out loud. Bittu loses both in the Janpath galis, opens the bag in a quiet lane: cash, the ledger, three pages torn out.
- CUTSCENE: Meera's cameo through the windscreen (8 s). The bag opened under a streetlight (20 s).
- RADIO: news bulletin: a man found dead near Janpath. RJ Munna, after it: "Lo, bata diya."
- AMBIENT: the jeep's scanner: "DL 1RA 0610, auto, green-yellow."
- INNER: "Bittu, tu sirf auto chalata hai. Sirf auto."

**M03 Barricade at ITO (Cop).** Meera's first mission. The dead man is her case. A radio call: the stolen dark car is fleeing from CP. Pursuit over the ITO flyovers, a barricade at the next junction, the driver (Gullu) crashes and fires from behind the car: a short standoff and exchange of fire, then a foot chase and takedown. In the car: a slip with an auto number she already knows and "Danny, Chandni Chowk". Khanna on the radio: "Shabaash, beta. Ghar jaake so jaana."
- CUTSCENE: the station, Meera tying her hair, Ramphal with two chais (25 s). The arrest close-up (15 s).
- PHONE: none yet; the police radio carries Khanna.
- INNER: "Stolen car. Two men. One got out at CP. Who drives this calm?"
- AMBIENT: Ramphal: "Madam, chai thandi ho gayi, aur aap garam."

**M05 Lead to the Chowk (Cop).** Meera takes the bike to the Chowk and walks the bazaar. The shopkeeper the munshi was meant to meet is terrified. A man tails her; a tail chase through the galis, a short takedown. The shopkeeper whispers the name Danny. Khanna calls: "Upar se pressure hai. Dheere."
- DRIVE TALK: Ramphal on the bike radio, gossiping about Khanna.
- PHONE: Khanna's call.
- INNER: "Danny. Not a name. A rumour with a jacket."
- AMBIENT: shopkeepers: "Policewali aayi thi, bag ke baare mein."

**M06 First favour (Fixer, past story).** Sepia flashback, fifteen years ago. Boy Danny takes his first job from Jaggi Chacha: carry a sealed package across the Chowk. Rule: never ask what is inside. He slips through the crowd, shakes a tail, wins one scrappy fight. He bumps into a boy with a steel tiffin and catches it. The boy runs off crying for it. Danny hands the package to a big quiet man (young Bhola) at a godown door. A wall calendar shows 5 October. Nothing else is shown.
- CUTSCENE: Jaggi giving the rule, a coin pressed into Danny's palm (40 s).
- INNER (adult Danny, over the flashback): "Pehli kamai. Bees rupaye. Sasta tha."
- AMBIENT: a radio in a shop playing an old song (the "factory song", reused in Act 3 and 5).

**M04 Chandni Chowk pickup (Fixer).** Present day. Jaggi passes Danny a job from a client he calls only Madam: get the bag back. The shop is shuttered; the shopkeeper's boy gives him the auto number and "a policewali was here". A crew jumps him in the lane: fistfight, police whistles, wanted 1, a scooter escape.
- PHONE: Madam's first call. A woman's voice, calm, filtered: "Bag chahiye. Andar kya hai, mat poochna." Danny: "Main kabhi nahi poochta."
- INNER: "Five lakh. Dead courier. Price just went up."
- AMBIENT: the crowd: "Bhaago, bhaago."

**M07 Wrong auto (Fixer, crossing).** Danny finds Bittu at the station stand. A tense talk by the auto, all smiles. Meera pulls up; she traced the number too. Stand-off in the forecourt; nobody says what they know. Danny sees the stopped watch, 6:10, and his smile slips for one frame. He walks into the station crowd and is gone.
- CUTSCENE (big): the three in one shot for the first time, a train announcement for 6:10 over them (90 s).
- INNER: "Watch. 6:10. No." Then the inner voice stops for the rest of the scene.
- AMBIENT: station announcements.

**M08 Nowhere to hide (Driver).** Bittu takes the bag to Ustad Rafiq at the CP stand. Rafiq reads the ledger's shorthand: "Yeh hisaab hai, beta. Pandrah saal ka." The crew arrives and shoves Rafiq around. Bittu drives him out through the back lanes. In the bag's lining Bittu finds a torn corner: "Daryaganj, Itwaar". The first missing page clue.
- DRIVE TALK: Rafiq about Bittu's father: "Ramesh bhi aise hi muskurata tha jab dar lagta tha."
- INNER: "Not my problem. Not my... okay, thoda sa."
- CUTSCENE: the torn corner found (15 s).

**M09 Khanna says drop it (Cop).** Chai with Khanna near ITO. He is warm, funny, tells her about her first day. Then: drop the case. She learns Gullu was released this morning, on Khanna's signature. That night she tails the crew's car, lights off, toward Old Delhi, and overhears the meeting spot: Red Fort parking.
- CUTSCENE: Khanna buttering her toast, asking about her mother (45 s).
- DRIVE TALK: Ramphal in the passenger seat, nervous: "Madam, ACP saab ko pata chala toh..."
- INNER: "He signed it. He signs everything. Doesn't mean anything." It does.

**M10 Red Fort rush (merged finale, all three).** Bittu runs with the bag down Netaji Subhas Marg, a crew car on him. Switch to Danny in the Red Fort parking: pistol fight with Bhola's men. Switch to Meera: the crew leader flees by car, chase, ram, arrest. Then all three on foot under the red walls. Meera wants the ledger as evidence. Danny wants his fee. Bittu wants out. Sirens: Rawat's team. Meera reads one line in the ledger: her own thana's name. She lets them go. Bittu keeps the ledger, Danny takes the cash, Meera pockets the munshi's phone from the bag lining. It rings. A woman's voice: "Gurugram mein milte hain."
- CUTSCENE (big): the stand-off under the walls, then the phone (2 min).
- INNER: all three in sequence on the switch, one line each.
- **Act-ending hook:** the call. Then a cut to a Gurugram tower at night, a man in a glass office (Sethi) turning off a TV.

**Turning points:** M02 (the bag is a death sentence), M07 (they see each other), M10 (Meera breaks a rule).
**Set pieces:** CP circle chase; ITO flyover pursuit and gunfight; three-way relay at Red Fort.

### Act 2: Paisa (14 missions)

**Theme:** money has a smell. Charity, towers, gig apps, GB Road: it all ends in one ledger.
**Places:** Laxmi Nagar, Daryaganj, GB Road, Kashmere Gate ISBT, Gurugram (Cyber City, the towers, the expressway), Delhi Gymkhana, the Seva hospital.
**Opening image:** Sonu's delivery bike under a Metro pillar at dawn. Above him a Sethi hoarding, "Dilli Ka Dil", with a pigeon on the S.
**Big cutscenes (4):** Bittu's family at dinner (M11); Rani's escape and the 6:10 tickets (M17); Sethi's gala speech (M22); the vault and Kabir (M24), plus the act-end hook.

**M11 Ghar (Driver).** Laxmi Nagar. Amma, Sonu, dinner, a TV serial. Bhola's men are at the Metro pillar. A chase under the Blue Line with Sonu riding pillion. Back home, Amma's hospital card: Sethi Seva Trust. Bittu looks at "H.S." in the ledger.
- CUTSCENE (big): dinner, everyone talking over each other (75 s).
- DRIVE TALK: Sonu about reels, his ratings, "Bhaiya, algorithm ne aaj phir kaat diya."
- INNER: "H.S. Hospital, Sethi. Nahi. Bahut log ka naam H se shuru hota hai."

**M12 Paper trail (Cop).** Khanna puts Meera on desk duty. She takes the munshi's phone to Ira Bose, who traces the last call to a Gurugram tower. First drive to Gurugram at night: the glass city opens up. A courier on a bike sees her and runs: pursuit on the expressway.
- PHONE: Ira on speaker.
- AMBIENT: Gurugram guards, gig riders at the gate.
- INNER: "Delhi se 30 km. Alag shehar. Same ledger."

**M13 Madam (Fixer).** A Cyber City rooftop bar. Danny's client hands over through Kabir Sethi, all charm and nerves. Madam on the phone: double the fee for the ledger. Bhola's men are in the bar too. A fight down the parking ramps.
- PHONE: Madam. "Danny, tumhe sab kuch bikta hai na? Toh bik jao."
- DRIVE TALK: Kabir, drunk, in Danny's passenger seat: "My father funds hospitals. I fund this bar. Who's the bad guy?"

**M14 Daryaganj Sunday (Driver).** The book market. Bittu searches stalls with Rafiq's hint while Bhola's men search too. A chase through the stalls and Daryaganj lanes. The mission ends with the stall owner saying, "Ek panna? Haan, kisi ne bechne ko diya tha." The page itself is optional (Page 1 hunt, section 9).
- DRIVE TALK: Rafiq teaching Bittu to read three symbols of the shorthand.
- RADIO: RJ Munna: "Itwaar ko kitaabein sasti milti hain. Sach mehenga."

**M15 GB Road raid (Cop).** Meera raids traffickers above the hardware shops, chasing the munshi's last night. She clears the stairs, gets the people held there out safe, and finds Rani, who knew him.
- CUTSCENE: Rani on the stairs, refusing to be "rescued" by force: "Main khud chalungi." (30 s)
- AMBIENT: shopkeepers below, shutters coming down.
- INNER: "Address in a dead man's phone. Fourteen women upstairs. One named him."

**M16 GB Road money (Fixer).** Danny deals with the money men who run the street. Their ledger shows a cut going to Bhola every month and donations to the Seva Trust. He takes the ledger page by charm, then by a fight.

## 8. Endings

All four start at 6:10 on the rampart with the phone ringing. Sethi is there. What each costs:

**Expose (Meera's way).** Meera takes the ledger, the records, the CCTV and the report to Ira's van. The pages go out live on the city's screens on results morning. Sethi is arrested on the rampart, on camera. The city erupts: crowds on Kartavya Path, traffic stopped for a day, nobody against anybody, everyone against the ledger. Cost: Meera's career (jail on the Shoot path, no badge on the Refuse path). The Seva hospital shuts; Amma's treatment stops; Tara loses everything. Danny is named in the ledger ("J. ka ladka") and is arrested or runs. M is named in nobody's report.

**Sell (Danny's way).** Danny answers the phone and sells the ledger to Madam for crores. Sethi takes the oath. Dilli 2040 goes on. The Bela basti is gone by spring. Sonu is freed, Bittu's family gets a flat, Bittu owns his auto. Bittu never speaks to Danny again. Danny in Dubai, alone, opens the tiffin. Cost: the city. In the Shoot path, the last shot is a new ledger page: "M.R., first entry."

**Burn (Bittu's way).** Bittu tears out one page, his father's, and burns the rest on the rampart. He gives the page to Sethi and says: withdraw, fund the basti, and this stays a page. Sethi resigns "for health" a week later. The system survives; Khanna's replacement smiles at Meera. The family is safe. Danny leaves Delhi on the 6:10 train. Cost: the truth, buried. His father is never named in public.

**Aakhri Panna (the hidden fourth).** Needs: all three missing pages found and Danny forgiven. The three pages complete the last page of the ledger: fourteen names, and the fourteenth reads "Shanti, laash nahi mili" (no body found). Under it, the M signature, and a tear exactly where a name was written beside it. The three go to RJ Munna's empty studio and read the names live on 106.1 at 6:10, then the payments, then the initials. Danny reads his own line first: "J. ka ladka. Taala maine lagaya." Meera arrests him on air. Sethi falls. Khanna (if alive) is named. M is not. The munshi's phone rings one last time. A woman's voice: "Shukriya." Then M67, the last fare. Cost: Danny's freedom, by his own choice. M walks.

## 11. Controversy, done right

The story critiques systems. Every person, party, police force, gang, channel and company is invented. No community, caste or religion is a villain or a victim group. No cruelty for fun.

| System | How the story critiques it | Where | The line we do not cross |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demolition for a "world-class city" | The Bela basti, the bulldozer morning, people who keep the city running pushed to its edge | Act 4, M39, M46 | Basti people are full characters with jobs, jokes and plans. No one is hurt for spectacle. Children flee and are never in danger. |
| Fake encounters | A cop asked to kill a guilty, cuffed man to free an innocent one. Both choices cost. | M32, M33, M47, M49 | The shot is off camera. No torture. The police are individuals, good and bad; the force is not mocked. |
| Media trials | Prime time convicts Sonu before a court does; the edit is fake | M28, M29 | Rocky is a format, not a caricature of a real person. Invented channel. |
| Charity as a laundromat | The hospital saves lives and launders money at once. Both are true. | M20, M43, M48 | The hospital's good is real; patients and nurses are never the joke. |
| Gig work | Sonu and the Jhatpat riders, ratings, blame | M11, M21, chain 6 | Invented app. Riders are heroes of their own scenes. |
| Elections and money | An invented party, a candidate funded from the ledger, a win anyway | M22, M62 | Invented party (Nav Dilli Dal), no real symbol, colour or slogan. |
| Trafficking around GB Road | The crime around the street: the cop raids traffickers, the fixer follows the money, the driver gets someone out | M15, M16, M17, chain 11 | GB Road decision: hardware shops below, background upstairs, dignity always, no sex, no nudity, no paying for sex as a mechanic. Rani chooses her own way out. |
| Drugs in East Delhi | A den raid that shows sick young men, not glamour | M40 | No how-to, no glamour. |
| Compensation for silence | Amma signed; most families did. Was she wrong? | M43 | She is never blamed by the story, only by Bittu, for one scene. |

Names: villains and allies come from many backgrounds; surnames are used sparingly so no group reads as the villain. Places of worship appear only where they really are and are never combat spaces (no missions inside Nizamuddin's dargah area or any temple; chain 9 waits outside).

Scenes people will argue about, by design: the encounter (M32), the playable lock (M42: the game makes you lock the door), Amma's settlement (M43), Sethi's "kiraayedar" line (M65), the Sell ending's "M.R." page, and whether the Malcha woman was ever there.
