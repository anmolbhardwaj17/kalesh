## How the pieces fit

The game is a Godot 4.7.2 project in `game/`. Since P1-024 the game starts in `scenes/city.tscn` (scene id `city`): the streamed pilot centre with the player in an auto at a spawn pin (`game/data/spawn_pins.json`, `--spawn=<pin>`) and PlayerMode. `scenes/main.tscn` (id `scaffold`) is the flat stand-in scene (ground, road strip, north post, V2 car, chase camera, PlayerMode) kept for the quick driving checks. The V2 car (`vehicles/vehicle.gd`) reads input and steps on `SimClock.sim_tick` (a fixed 1/60 s tick), the chase camera (`camera/chase_camera.gd`) follows. With the user arg `--debug`, `GameArgs` adds the debug server (`game/debug/`) that serves `window.__game` style hooks over TCP to the harness. The earlier three.js scaffold (`src/`, `index.html`, Vite) was retired in P0-027; `tools/geo/` holds the reference projection for the offline bake.

The architecture was signed on 2026-11-11 (TECH.md). Data flows one way: pinned sources in `data/` and hand-edited data in `harness/data/` go through the offline bake in `tools/bake/` (Node and TypeScript), which writes 250 m `KTL1` tiles and reports; the game streams the tiles and never reads raw sources; the map inspector (`tools/inspector/`) reads the same tiles and writes override files back to `harness/data/`. The section "Target module map" is the signed layout the production code grows into; "Source map" lists what exists today.

## Target module map (signed 2026-11-11)

Rules for every module:
- No file owns more than one of streaming, building, physics and cleanup (the old game's 4,065 line tile manager). A GDScript file over 600 lines or a TypeScript file over 800 lines fails the module size test (`game/tests/test_module_size.gd`, `tools/module_size.test.ts`, since P1-006; over-limit Phase 0 files sit on a named exception list with their retiring task).
- Every streamed system registers `build(tile)` and `dispose(tile)` with the streaming core and has its own budget (perf_budgets.json `systems`). The streaming core knows nothing about what a system builds.
- Every stepping system connects to `SimClock.sim_tick`; nothing steps in its own `_physics_process`.
- The bake is pure functions plus one driver: no file or network access below `tools/bake/main.ts` and `tools/bake/sources/`.
- One implementation per algorithm: the projection, the lane model, the seeded random generator and the tile codec each exist once in the bake; the game has only readers.

### Driving (V2, P1-020)
The player's car in the main scene and the course is the V2 vehicle (`game/vehicles/vehicle.gd` with the tune `game/data/vehicles/car.tres`), followed by `game/camera/chase_camera.gd`. Measured numbers (feel probes in sim ticks on the course): 0 to 100 km/h 11.1 s, top speed 140.0 km/h, 100 to 0 in 41.5 m, full lock radius 6.9 m at 37 km/h, one-sided kerb at 40 km/h 6.1 degrees of roll, all wheels on the flyover deck joints at 100 km/h, input answered in 1 tick, 0.02 ms per car per tick among 16. Verified by `car_accel`, `car_top`, `car_brake`, `car_lock`, `car_kerb`, `car_flyover`, `car_cost`, `chase_cam_floor`, `drive_north`, `drive_turn_right` and `game/tests/test_vehicle.gd`; the debug probes are `vehicle(cmd, opts)` (`game/debug/vehicle_probe.gd`). The look stand-in scenes (`scenes/standin/builder.gd`) use the same car (the car tune with the kit `hatch` body, tune `body` hatch or sedan) and chase camera since P1-044, on one flat ground collider; the kinematic car is removed. The course flyover ramps end in a 40 m crest vertical curve (radius 667 m), so the car stays on the ground over the top at 100 km/h. P1-021 adds the auto (`auto.tres`: top 65.4 km/h, 0 to 40 in 5.35 s, 60 to 0 in 19.2 m, full lock radius 4.03 m at 15 km/h, 1.1 degrees of roll at full lock from 40 km/h, through the course gali's 3 m corner with 0.26 m to spare) and the scooter (`scooter.tres`: top 90.0 km/h, 0 to 60 in 5.6 s, 80 to 0 in 28.4 m, upright through a 40 km/h slalom with the body leaning up to 40 degrees), checked by `auto_*`, `scooter_*`, `chase_cam_auto` and `chase_cam_scooter`; the longer probes `gali` and `stable` are in `game/debug/vehicle_routes.gd`. ### On foot (P1-022)

### Humanoid (P1-023)
Every on-foot character draws a humanoid (`game/characters/humanoid_anim.gd`); the capsule of `on_foot.gd` stays the collision shape and is drawn only when the rig files are missing. The rig is the Quaternius Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 (CC0), retargeted to Godot's `SkeletonProfileHumanoid` by the import bone map `game/characters/rig/ual_bone_map.tres`: `tools/characters/ual_build.mjs` downloads the two free packs into the gitignored `data/ual/` (pinned in `data/SOURCES.json`), imports the .glb files in a temporary gitignored `game/characters/ual_src/` and runs `game/characters/tools/build_humanoid.gd`, which writes the only committed outputs: `rig/ual_clips.res` (14 clips, their loop modes and the stride speed of each moving loop) and `rig/mannequin_m.scn` and `mannequin_f.scn` (skeleton and one skinned mesh carrying body region and rest position per vertex). One AnimationNodeTransition (cross fade 0.15 s) and a TimeScale drive idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, air, land, vault, sit enter, sit, sit exit and drive; the tree is advanced on the sim tick by the character. A moving clip's rate is ground speed / (stride x body scale) (`foot_contact.gd`), so the planted foot stays put. The three leads are one mannequin each (the female one for Meera) dressed by `rig/lead_look.gdshader` from `leads.gd` (clothes painted from the rest pose, so nothing pokes through the body; Meera's bun is a sphere on the head bone). `PlayerMode` plays sit enter at the door and sit exit after getting out. Measured: foot slide 0.5 percent at walk and 3.1 at jog (7.3 at sprint), animation 0.017 ms per character among 16. Licence `rig/LICENSE_UAL.txt`, credit line `game/data/credits.json`. Verified by `game/tests/test_humanoid.gd` and the scenarios `foot_anim` and `foot_anim_shots`.

### Stand-in scenes
`game/scenes/standin/builder.gd` builds a 300 m six lane arterial (median, kerbs, service lane, signal junction, flyover ramp stub, bus stop, about 80 shop houses with labelled boards, 21 static vehicles, 28 people, the player car with the chase camera and HUD), a landmark row (hero arch, signature domed tomb, marker block, each with surroundings) and two area kit blocks (bazaar strip, Lutyens road). Static geometry is merged per block and material (about 5 meshes, 100 to 175 draw calls with shadows). Look proof views in `harness/look_proof/poses.json`; scenarios `street_boot`, `landmarks_boot`, `areas_boot`.

### Streaming (P1-019)
`game/world/world_stream.gd` puts the streamed city into a scene (`--tiles=test` or `--tiles=full`): it opens the tile index, makes the streaming core (`game/world/streaming/stream_core.gd`) and registers the four systems of `game/world/systems/`. Each frame the core follows the player, asks `ring_plan.gd` which tiles are wanted (within 375 m of the player or of the point 2 s ahead), reads new tiles on WorkerThreadPool tasks (`tile_reader.gd`, where each system also prepares its part), and lets `attach_queue.gd` run the systems' attach and dispose steps for at most 1 ms: meshes first, then collider chunks 3 a frame, never both in one frame. Tiles past 500 m are disposed. A load or a teleport (`warp`) runs the queue in a burst until the ring is live. A bad tile is reported and skipped. TECH.md "Streaming (ST1, TL1)" has the rules and the measured numbers. Verified by `game/tests/test_streaming.gd` and the scenarios `tiles_test_block_drive` (quick), `stream_teleport`, `stream_200`, `stream_140` and `stream_memory` (the last four need the full pilot tiles).

### Debug hooks
Godot: `game/debug/debug_server.gd` serves every hook plus shot, quality and quit over TCP JSON lines on 127.0.0.1 (docs/GAME_HOOKS.md "Godot transport"); `GameArgs` adds it only with `--debug`. Sim time comes from `SimClock`, events from `GameEvents`. Verified by `game/tests/test_debug.gd` and `harness/bin/godotsmoke.mjs`.
The hook contract is checked on every `verify --quick` by the `hooks_contract` scenario.
